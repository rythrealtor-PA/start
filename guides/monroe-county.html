Living in Monroe County, PA: A Local Guide for Home Buyers

By Ryth Vara, REALTOR® · Real of Pennsylvania · Updated October 8, 2026

In short: Monroe County is the heart of the Pocono Mountains in northeastern Pennsylvania. Its county seat is Stroudsburg, it is served by I-80 and I-380, and it is about 1.5 to 2 hours from New York City by car. Homes range from historic borough houses to lake and resort communities, many of which belong to a property owners' association (POA).

Where Monroe County is

Monroe County sits in northeastern Pennsylvania along the Delaware River, across from New Jersey. It is the center of the Pocono Mountains region, home to the Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area, Big Pocono State Park, and ski resorts such as Camelback Mountain in Tannersville and Shawnee Mountain near East Stroudsburg.

Getting around

  • I-80 crosses the county east to west, toward New Jersey and New York City.
  • I-380 runs north toward Scranton, and Route 33 runs south to the Lehigh Valley.
  • From Stroudsburg, approximate drive times are about 50 minutes to Allentown, 1.5 to 2 hours to New York City, and about 2 hours to Philadelphia. Traffic and season make a real difference.
  • Commuter bus service to New York City runs from the Stroudsburg area. Ask Ryth about current routes and schedules.

Towns Ryth serves in Monroe County

  • Stroudsburg: the county seat, with a walkable Main Street and historic homes.
  • Tannersville: home of Camelback Mountain and The Crossings Premium Outlets, right off I-80.
  • Pocono Pines: lake communities in the higher Poconos, near Lake Naomi.
  • Saylorsburg: the West End along Route 33, close to Ryth's office in Brodheadsville.

Types of homes

  • Historic homes in the boroughs of Stroudsburg and East Stroudsburg
  • Single-family homes on wooded lots in the surrounding townships
  • Lake and resort communities with private amenities, usually run by a POA
  • Vacation homes and cabins, including homes used as short-term rentals
  • Luxury homes on large lots and acreage
  • Land and building lots

Property taxes

In Pennsylvania, property taxes go to three bodies: the county, the municipality (the township or borough) and the school district. Each sets its own rate, so two similar homes a few miles apart can have very different tax bills.

Monroe County has four school districts: East Stroudsburg Area, Pleasant Valley, Pocono Mountain and Stroudsburg Area. Before you make an offer, Ryth will get you the actual tax bill for the home, not an estimate.

What to check before you buy

  • POA rules and fees. Ask for the community's rules, annual dues and any special assessments. Pennsylvania law requires the seller to provide a resale certificate for homes in planned communities.
  • Well and septic. Many homes here are not on public water or sewer, so a well test and a septic inspection are worth doing.
  • Radon. Radon is common in Pennsylvania, and testing during the inspection period is standard practice.
  • Short-term rental rules, if you plan to rent the home. They differ by township and community. See Investing in a Pocono vacation rental.
  • Flood zones near creeks and rivers, and whether flood insurance is required.
  • Winter access. Find out who plows private and community roads.

What clients say

Questions

Is Monroe County the same as the Poconos?

Mostly. The Pocono Mountains region covers four counties (Monroe, Pike, Wayne and Carbon), and Monroe County is at its center.

Do I need a car to live in Monroe County?

In most of the county, yes. Local bus service is limited, so most residents drive, and many commuters to New York City use I-80 or the commuter buses.
