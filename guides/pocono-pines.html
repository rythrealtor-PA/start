Living in Pocono Pines, PA: Lake Communities and Second Homes

By Ryth Vara, REALTOR® · Real of Pennsylvania · Updated October 8, 2026

In short: Pocono Pines is a lake community in Tobyhanna Township, Monroe County, in the higher elevations of the Poconos around Lake Naomi and Pocono Lake. It is reached by Route 940, a few miles from I-380, and many homes are vacation or second homes in private lake communities. It is served by the Pocono Mountain School District.

Living in Pocono Pines

Pocono Pines is lake country. Several private communities around Lake Naomi and Pinecrest Lake offer amenities such as beaches, boating, pools and golf, and many homeowners use their homes as weekend and vacation retreats. Pocono Raceway in nearby Long Pond, resort water parks and ski areas are all a short drive away.

Getting around

  • Route 940 runs through Pocono Pines and connects to I-380 a few miles away, which leads to I-80 and Scranton.
  • New York City is about 100 miles away, roughly 2 hours by car.
  • Stroudsburg is about 30 minutes away, and Scranton about 40 minutes.

Homes in Pocono Pines

  • Lake homes and lake-view homes, some with lake rights
  • Homes in private communities with clubhouses and shared amenities
  • Cabins and chalets
  • Luxury homes on larger wooded lots

What to check before you buy

  • Lake access. Whether a home includes lake rights, and on what terms, depends on the community. Get it in writing.
  • Community dues, rules and the resale certificate, including any rules on renting the home.
  • Private roads and winter plowing.
  • Well, septic and radon inspections.
  • Tobyhanna Township's short-term rental rules, if you plan to rent the home.

What clients say

Questions

Do people buy second homes in Pocono Pines?

Yes. Many homes in the lake communities are weekend and vacation homes, and some owners rent them out when they are not using them, where the township and community allow it.

Can I rent my Pocono Pines home short-term?

It depends on Tobyhanna Township's current rules and on the rules of the specific community, which can be stricter. Ryth confirms both before you make an offer.

What school district serves Pocono Pines?

Pocono Pines is in Tobyhanna Township, which is served by the Pocono Mountain School District. Confirm the district for the exact address.
