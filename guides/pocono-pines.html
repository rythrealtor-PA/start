Living in Pocono Pines, PA: Lake Naomi and Lake Homes

By Ryth Vara, REALTOR® · Real of Pennsylvania · Updated October 9, 2026

In short: Living in Pocono Pines means lake life in the higher part of the Poconos. Lake Naomi is the big attraction, with the private Lake Naomi Club for boating, pools, tennis, pickleball, golf and community events, and woods and quiet roads all around. Pocono Pines is in Tobyhanna Township on Route 940, a few minutes from I-380, close to Pocono Raceway and Kalahari Resorts, and about 2 hours from New York City.

Where Pocono Pines is

Pocono Pines is a village in Tobyhanna Township, on Route 940 between Mount Pocono and Blakeslee. A Pocono Pines mailing address (ZIP 18350) can also be in Coolbaugh Township, to the north.

Homes and communities

  • Lake homes and lake-view homes
  • Homes in private communities with clubhouses and shared amenities
  • Cabins and chalets in the woods
  • Luxury homes on larger wooded lots

Lake Naomi and the Lake Naomi Club

Pocono Pines is known for established lake living, and Lake Naomi is the biggest attraction in the area. The private Lake Naomi Club runs the lake activities, pools, sports, fitness and community events.

Not every home in Pocono Pines comes with a club membership. The club offers membership to property owners in Lake Naomi and Timber Trails, and an owner who wants to join applies and pays the club's fees. For 2026, the club lists a one-time $5,000 application fee and an $8,000 initial capital contribution (or a five-year payment plan), plus yearly dues of $3,980 to $5,970 depending on the membership type. Paid in full, the first year comes to about $17,000 to $19,000.

Owners in Lake Naomi also pay a yearly assessment to the Pocono Pines Community Association ($1,058 for a house in 2026), whether or not they join the club. Timber Trails is a gated community with its own association fee ($2,890 for a house in 2026).

Ryth's take

Getting around

  • Route 940 runs through Pocono Pines, and I-380 is a few minutes east at Pocono Summit.
  • Pocono Raceway in Long Pond and Kalahari Resorts in Pocono Manor are a short drive away.
  • Stroudsburg is about 30 minutes away, Scranton about 40 minutes and New York City about 2 hours.

What to check before you buy

  • The community's rules and policies, including any limits on renting. Ryth gets them for you before you make an offer.
  • Lake Naomi Club membership. Confirm the home is eligible, and budget for the application fee, the capital contribution and the yearly dues.
  • Lake access, in writing. Whether a home has lake rights depends on the community.
  • Private roads and winter plowing.
  • Well, septic and radon inspections.
  • Rental rules, if you plan to rent. Tobyhanna Township licenses short-term rentals for up to 2 guests per bedroom plus 2. In Lake Naomi, renters can use the club only through its Temporary Membership program: the owner has to join at the family level, pay a $175 yearly rental registration fee and meet the club's minimum stay.

What clients say

Questions

Does every home in Pocono Pines come with a Lake Naomi Club membership?

No. Membership is offered to property owners in Lake Naomi and Timber Trails, and it is optional and paid separately. For 2026, the first year costs about $17,000 to $19,000 paid in full: a $5,000 application fee, an $8,000 initial capital contribution, and yearly dues of $3,980 to $5,970.

Can I rent my Pocono Pines home short-term?

Tobyhanna Township allows short-term rentals with a license. The community can be stricter: in Lake Naomi, renters can use the club only through a Temporary Membership, with a minimum stay. Ryth confirms both before you make an offer.

What school district serves Pocono Pines?

Pocono Pines is in Tobyhanna Township, which is served by the Pocono Mountain School District. Confirm the district for the exact address.
