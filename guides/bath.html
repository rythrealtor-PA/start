Living in Bath, PA: A Small Borough in the Northern Lehigh Valley

By Ryth Vara, REALTOR® · Real of Pennsylvania · Updated October 8, 2026

In short: Bath is a small borough in Northampton County, in the northern Lehigh Valley west of Nazareth. It has a compact small-town center and is surrounded by townships with single-family homes, newer developments and farmland, with Bethlehem, Allentown and Easton each about 20 to 30 minutes away. Bath is served by the Northampton Area School District.

Living in Bath

Bath offers small-town living with the larger Lehigh Valley cities close by. The borough itself is compact, with older homes near the center, while the surrounding townships offer more space, newer construction and rural land.

Getting around

  • Route 248 and Route 512 run through the area, with quick access to Route 22 and Route 33.
  • Bethlehem and Allentown are about 20 to 25 minutes away, and Easton about 25 to 30.
  • Stroudsburg and the Poconos are about 40 minutes north via Route 33.

Homes in and around Bath

  • Older homes in the borough
  • Single-family homes and new construction in townships such as East Allen, Moore and Upper Nazareth
  • Farmhouses, acreage and land

What to check before you buy

  • The municipality and school district. Bath-area addresses fall in several townships, and taxes and the school district depend on the exact address.
  • Well and septic for homes outside the borough.
  • Zoning, if you are buying land to build on or want room for animals or a workshop.
  • The resale inspection the municipality may require at sale.

What clients say

Questions

Where is Bath, PA?

Bath is in Northampton County, in the northern part of the Lehigh Valley, west of Nazareth and north of Bethlehem.

What school district serves Bath?

The Borough of Bath is served by the Northampton Area School District. Some surrounding addresses are in other districts, such as Nazareth Area, so confirm the district for the exact address.

Is Bath close to the Poconos?

Yes. Route 33 north reaches I-80 and Stroudsburg in about 40 minutes.
