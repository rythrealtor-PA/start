Investing in Lehigh Valley Real Estate: Rentals, 2 to 4 Units and Fix-and-Flip in Allentown, Bethlehem and Easton

By Ryth Vara, REALTOR® · Real of Pennsylvania · Updated October 13, 2026

In short: The Lehigh Valley's three cities, Allentown, Bethlehem and Easton, are its strongest rental markets, close to jobs at the region's hospitals, universities, warehouses and manufacturers. From October 2025 to September 2026, the median rent for a 3-bedroom home was about $1,900 in Allentown, $2,000 in Bethlehem and $1,958 in Easton, and 2 to 4 unit buildings sold for medians of $329,000, $378,000 and $388,500, according to the Greater Lehigh Valley REALTORS® MLS. All three cities register or license rentals, and Allentown's transfer tax is 2.5%.

Where the jobs are

Renters follow the jobs, and the Lehigh Valley's largest private employers, as listed by the Lehigh Valley Economic Development Corporation, include:

  • Hospitals: Lehigh Valley Health Network and St. Luke's University Health Network
  • Warehouses and online retail: Amazon, along with the logistics centers along I-78
  • Manufacturing: Mack Trucks, Air Products, B. Braun Medical, Lutron Electronics, Crayola, Victaulic and Olympus
  • Colleges: Lehigh University in Bethlehem and Lafayette College in Easton
  • Others: Guardian Life Insurance and Wind Creek's casino resort in Bethlehem

Allentown, Bethlehem and Easton sit in the middle of these jobs, with bus service, older multifamily buildings and the most rentals.

City rules for landlords

  • Bethlehem: every rental unit needs a yearly license and is inspected every three years, and a unit rented to 3 to 5 unrelated people needs a separate regulated rental license.
  • Easton: residential rental units are registered every year and inspected regularly.
  • Allentown: rental units must be registered with the city.
  • All three inspect a property before it is sold, so read the pre-sale inspection report before you make an offer; it shows what the city wants fixed.
  • Older buildings: rentals built before 1978 need a lead-based paint disclosure to every tenant.

Fix-and-flip in the Lehigh Valley

The cities have many older rowhomes, twins and single-family homes, and updated homes sell quickly: homes in Allentown, Bethlehem and Easton spent an average of 19 to 26 days on the market in the same 12 months. What decides a flip:

  • The purchase price, against recent sales of updated homes on the same kind of street
  • The city's inspection report, which lists required repairs, plus the inspection you will need again when you sell
  • Historic districts. In Bethlehem's, exterior changes go to the Historic and Architectural Review Board before the city issues a permit.
  • Older systems: wiring, plumbing, roofs and lead paint
  • The transfer tax: 2.5% in total in Allentown, usually 2% elsewhere, paid on both the purchase and the sale

Distressed properties come up through county sheriff sales, county tax sales, bank-owned listings and estate sales. In Pennsylvania, a tax upset sale can leave recorded mortgages and liens on the property, while a judicial sale clears them, so get a title search and see an attorney before you bid.

Financing

  • Live in one unit of a 2 to 4 unit building, and you can use owner-occupied loans, including FHA loans, which allow 1 to 4 units when you live in one of them, with a smaller down payment than an investment loan.
  • Investment loans go up to 85% of the price for a single-family home (about 15% down) and 75% for 2 to 4 units (about 25% down) under Fannie Mae rules.
  • Loans based on the rent qualify you on the property's expected income instead of your own, usually with a larger down payment.

What clients say

Questions

Is Allentown a good place to buy a rental property?

It can be. Allentown had the most 2 to 4 unit sales of the three cities (79, at a median of $329,000) and a median 3-bedroom rent of about $1,900 from October 2025 to September 2026. Rentals must be registered with the city, the city inspects every property before it is sold, and the transfer tax is 2.5%, so count all of it in the numbers.

How much is rent in Bethlehem, PA?

From October 2025 to September 2026, the median rent was about $1,798 for a 2-bedroom home and $2,000 for a 3-bedroom home in the City of Bethlehem, according to the Greater Lehigh Valley REALTORS® MLS.

What do 2 to 4 unit buildings sell for in the Lehigh Valley?

Medians of $329,000 in Allentown, $378,000 in Bethlehem and $388,500 in Easton, for sales from October 2025 to September 2026, according to the Greater Lehigh Valley REALTORS® MLS.

Do I need a rental license in Bethlehem, Easton or Allentown?

Yes, in some form. Bethlehem licenses every rental unit yearly and inspects it every three years, Easton registers rental units yearly and inspects them, and Allentown requires rental units to be registered.
