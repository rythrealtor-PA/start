Living in Stroudsburg, PA: Homes, Getting Around and What to Know

By Ryth Vara, REALTOR® · Real of Pennsylvania · Updated October 8, 2026

In short: Stroudsburg is a walkable borough and the county seat of Monroe County, in the Pocono Mountains. It sits on I-80 next to the Delaware Water Gap, about 75 miles from New York City, and offers historic homes near Main Street plus single-family homes in the surrounding townships. The borough is served by the Stroudsburg Area School District.

Living in Stroudsburg

Stroudsburg's Main Street is the Poconos' most traditional downtown, with shops, restaurants and the Monroe County Courthouse. The borough borders East Stroudsburg, home to East Stroudsburg University, and together the two form the largest center in the county.

Outdoors, Stroudsburg is minutes from the Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area, where the Appalachian Trail crosses the Delaware River, and close to the Shawnee Mountain and Camelback ski areas.

Getting around

  • I-80 runs along the edge of town, with US Route 209 and Route 611 connecting the surrounding townships.
  • New York City is about 75 miles away, roughly 1.5 to 2 hours by car depending on traffic.
  • Allentown is about 50 minutes south by Route 33, and Scranton about 45 minutes north by I-380.
  • Commuter buses to New York City run from the area. Ask Ryth about current schedules.

Homes in and around Stroudsburg

  • Historic and Victorian homes on the borough's tree-lined streets
  • Single-family homes in Stroud Township and Hamilton Township
  • Condominiums and townhomes
  • Larger homes and lots on the outskirts

A "Stroudsburg" address is not always in the borough. Many homes with a Stroudsburg mailing address are in neighboring townships, which means different property taxes, services and sometimes a different school district. Ryth checks the actual municipality for every home.

What to check before you buy

  • Flood zones. Brodhead Creek and McMichael Creek run through the area, so check the flood map and whether flood insurance is required.
  • Older homes. Homes built before 1978 come with a lead-based paint disclosure, and older systems deserve a careful inspection.
  • Radon testing, which is standard practice in Pennsylvania.
  • The municipality and school district for the exact address.

What clients say

Questions

Is Stroudsburg the same as East Stroudsburg?

No. They are separate boroughs next to each other, with separate governments and school districts: Stroudsburg Area and East Stroudsburg Area.

How far is Stroudsburg from New York City?

About 75 miles, or roughly 1.5 to 2 hours by car. Many residents commute by car or by commuter bus.

Does a Stroudsburg address mean the home is in the borough?

Not necessarily. Many Stroudsburg mailing addresses are in surrounding townships. That affects taxes and school district, so always confirm the municipality.
