## Living in Tannersville

Tannersville is built around year-round recreation. **Camelback Mountain Resort** offers skiing and snowboarding in winter, plus an indoor water park and the Camelback Lodge, and **Big Pocono State Park** sits on the same mountain with trails and wide views. **The Crossings Premium Outlets** draw shoppers from across the region.

## Getting around

- **I-80** has its own Tannersville exit, which makes the town easy to reach from New Jersey and New York.
- **Route 611** runs through the village toward Stroudsburg, about 15 minutes away.
- New York City is about 85 miles away, roughly 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours by car.

## Homes in Tannersville

- Single-family homes on wooded lots
- Communities with a property owners' association (POA) and shared amenities
- Condos and townhomes near Camelback
- Vacation homes, including homes used as short-term rentals
- Larger and luxury homes with mountain views

## Short-term rentals in Tannersville

Because of Camelback and the outlets, many buyers here think about renting their home to visitors. Pocono Township regulates short-term rentals, and many communities have their own rules on top of the township's. **Confirm both before you buy.** See [Investing in a Pocono vacation rental](pocono-vacation-rental-investing.html).

## What to check before you buy

- **Township and POA rental rules,** if you plan to rent.
- **POA dues, rules and the resale certificate.**
- **Well, septic and radon** inspections.
- **Winter access** to the home and who plows the road.

## Questions

### What school district serves Tannersville?

Tannersville is in Pocono Township, which is served by the Pocono Mountain School District. Always confirm the district for the exact address.

### Can I rent my Tannersville home to vacationers?

It depends on Pocono Township's current short-term rental rules and on the rules of the community the home is in. Ryth checks both before you make an offer.

### How far is Tannersville from New York City?

About 85 miles, or roughly 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours by car via I-80.
