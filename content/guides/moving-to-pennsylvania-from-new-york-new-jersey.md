## Why people move from New York and New Jersey

- **More house for the budget.** The same budget usually buys more space and more land here than in the New York and North Jersey suburbs.
- **Lower income tax,** especially if you work in New Jersey or from home (see the comparison below).
- **The outdoors.** Lakes, ski slopes, state parks and the Delaware River in the Poconos; river trails, small cities and farmland in the Lehigh Valley.
- **Staying close.** Most of the Poconos and the Lehigh Valley are within about 1.5 to 2 hours of New York City by car.

## Commuting to New York and New Jersey

- **By car to New York City:** about 75 miles from Stroudsburg, 85 from Tannersville and 100 from Pocono Pines, by I-80; about 70 miles from Easton and 80 from Bethlehem, by I-78. Plan on 1.5 to 2 hours, more at rush hour.
- **To jobs in New Jersey:** I-80 runs from the Poconos straight into Warren, Sussex and Morris counties. I-78 and Route 22 run from the Lehigh Valley into Hunterdon, Somerset and Union counties.
- **By bus from the Poconos:** Martz Trailways runs buses to New York City from Stroudsburg and Mount Pocono.
- **By bus from the Lehigh Valley:** Trans-Bridge Lines runs from the Allentown Bus Terminal, the Bethlehem Transportation Center, Lehigh Valley International Airport, the William Penn Park & Ride and the Easton Intermodal Transportation Center to New York, with weekday service to Lower Manhattan from some stops.
- **Philadelphia** is about 1 hour 15 minutes from Bethlehem and about 2 hours from Stroudsburg.

## Taxes compared

| Tax | Pennsylvania | New York | New Jersey |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| State income tax | Flat 3.07% | Up to 10.9% | 1.4% to 10.75% |
| Local income tax | Local earned income tax, set by the municipality and school district | Up to 3.876% for New York City residents | None on individuals |
| Property tax | Varies by county, municipality and school district | Varies by county and town | Highest effective rate in the country, 2.23% of home value |

What this means for you depends on where you work:

- **You work in New Jersey.** Pennsylvania and New Jersey have a reciprocal agreement: New Jersey does not tax the wages of Pennsylvania residents, so you pay Pennsylvania's 3.07% instead of New Jersey's rate. If New Jersey tax was withheld, you file a New Jersey nonresident return to get it back.
- **You work in New York.** New York still taxes the wages you earn there. Pennsylvania gives you a credit for that tax, up to Pennsylvania's own 3.07%, so your total state tax on those wages stays at about New York's rate. You no longer pay New York City's resident income tax.
- **You work from home or locally.** You pay Pennsylvania's 3.07% plus the local earned income tax.

Property taxes depend on the exact home, so Ryth gets you the actual tax bill before you make an offer. Once the home is your main residence, Pennsylvania's homestead exclusion lowers your school taxes; apply to the county by March 1. A tax professional can look at your own situation.

## Which towns fit your move

- **Working in New Jersey along I-80:** Stroudsburg, East Stroudsburg and the towns around them are the closest to the Delaware Water Gap bridge.
- **Working in New Jersey along I-78:** Easton is right at the bridge, with Bethlehem about 20 minutes farther west.
- **Taking the bus to Manhattan:** the Stroudsburg area on Martz, or Allentown, Bethlehem and Easton on Trans-Bridge.
- **Working from home, with the lakes and mountains:** Pocono Pines and its lake communities, Tannersville near Camelback, and the wooded townships around them.
- **City life you can walk:** Bethlehem's historic downtown, Easton's Centre Square and Stroudsburg's Main Street.
- **A lower price point:** the medians in Allentown and Easton were below Bethlehem's and below both county medians in the table above.

## Outdoor life

- **The Poconos:** skiing at Camelback and Shawnee Mountain, the Appalachian Trail through the Delaware Water Gap, swimming beaches at Tobyhanna and Gouldsboro state parks, and canoeing and tubing on the Delaware River.
- **The Lehigh Valley:** the Delaware & Lehigh Trail along the rivers, Jacobsburg Environmental Education Center near Nazareth, and Bethlehem's Musikfest every August.

## Your first months in Pennsylvania

- **Driver's license:** get a Pennsylvania license within 60 days of becoming a resident.
- **Your car:** apply for a Pennsylvania title and registration within 20 days.
- **Homestead exclusion:** apply to the county by March 1 once the home is your main residence.
- **Local earned income tax:** if your employer is outside Pennsylvania and does not withhold it, you may need to pay it yourself. The state's Municipal Statistics site lists the rate and the tax collector for any address.

## Questions

### Is it cheaper to live in Pennsylvania than New Jersey?

For most people, yes. Pennsylvania's income tax is a flat 3.07% against New Jersey's rates of up to 10.75%, New Jersey has the highest property tax rate of any state, and home prices in the Poconos and the Lehigh Valley are generally lower than in North Jersey. Commuting costs and the local earned income tax are part of the math.

### Do I pay New Jersey income tax if I live in Pennsylvania and work in New Jersey?

No, not on your wages. Under the reciprocal agreement between the two states, New Jersey does not tax the wages of Pennsylvania residents, and you pay Pennsylvania's 3.07% instead. The agreement covers wages and other pay for work as an employee, not every kind of income.

### Do I pay New York taxes if I live in Pennsylvania and work in New York?

Yes, New York taxes the wages you earn in New York. Pennsylvania gives a credit for that tax, up to its own 3.07% rate, and you no longer pay New York City's resident income tax.

### How far are the Poconos from New York City?

About 75 miles from Stroudsburg to about 100 miles from Pocono Pines, or roughly 1.5 to 2 hours by car on I-80. Martz Trailways runs buses to New York City from Stroudsburg and Mount Pocono.

### Can I commute from the Lehigh Valley to New York by bus?

Yes. Trans-Bridge Lines runs buses to New York from Allentown, Bethlehem, Easton, Lehigh Valley International Airport and the William Penn Park & Ride, with weekday service to Lower Manhattan from some stops.

### How long do I have to get a Pennsylvania driver's license after I move?

60 days from the day you become a Pennsylvania resident. Your car needs a Pennsylvania title and registration within 20 days.
