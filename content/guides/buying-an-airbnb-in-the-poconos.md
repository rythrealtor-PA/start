## Best towns for an Airbnb in the Poconos

Ryth looks first where the township rules are easier and guests already want to stay:

- **Pocono Pines, Pocono Lake and Blakeslee** (Tobyhanna Township, green). Lake Naomi, Emerald Lakes and other lake communities, close to Pocono Raceway, Kalahari Resorts, and the Jack Frost and Big Boulder ski areas just over the Carbon County line.
- **Tobyhanna and Pocono Summit** (Coolbaugh Township, green). A Pocono Country Place and Stillwater Lake Estates, next to I-380, close to Kalahari, Mount Airy Casino Resort and Tobyhanna and Gouldsboro state parks.
- **Long Pond** (Tunkhannock Township, green). Minutes from Pocono Raceway, with part of Emerald Lakes and Indian Mountain Lakes.
- **Bushkill and the rest of Middle Smithfield Township** (green), just north of East Stroudsburg. Near the Delaware River and the Delaware Water Gap National Recreation Area, a short drive from New Jersey. In AirDNA's figures, the East Stroudsburg area had the highest average revenue in the Poconos.
- **Mountainhome, Canadensis and Cresco** (Barrett Township, green). Quieter wooded settings near Skytop Lodge, Buck Hill Falls and the waterfalls of the northern Poconos.
- **Penn Estates** (Stroud Township, yellow). Stroud Township makes an exception for this gated community near Stroudsburg, which has central water and sewer.

Each of these still needs the township license and the community's own rules checked for the exact home.

## Step 1: Check the rules first

This is the step that sinks the most deals. Before you make an offer, confirm three things.

- The township's ordinance. Most Pocono townships require a permit or license, an inspection, occupancy limits and a local contact person, and some allow rentals only in certain zones.
- The community's rules. Many POAs restrict or prohibit short-term rentals, or charge fees for them, even where the township allows them.
- What the license allows. Plan your numbers on the bedrooms and guests the license permits, not on the occupancy in the current listing.

Ryth checks both the township and the community rules before you commit.

## Step 2: Taxes you will collect and pay

- Pennsylvania's 6% hotel occupancy tax, on stays of fewer than 30 consecutive days.
- Monroe County's 3% hotel room excise tax, which the county applies to homes rented for overnight stays.
- Income tax on your rental income. A tax professional can also explain depreciation and, if you are selling another investment property, a 1031 exchange.

## Step 3: Location

Guests want to be close to everything the Poconos have, without a long drive from New Jersey or New York. That is why the homes near the big attractions book the most nights: Camelback Resort and The Crossings Premium Outlets in Tannersville, Kalahari Resorts in Pocono Manor, Pocono Raceway in Long Pond, Mount Airy Casino Resort near Mount Pocono, and the shopping areas around them. Easy access from I-80 and I-380 matters too.

Guests who come for nature look for homes near Big Pocono State Park, Tobyhanna State Park, Marshalls Falls Park in Smithfield Township, the Dunnfield Creek trailhead just across the Delaware Water Gap in New Jersey, and the many lakes where they can fish, swim, go boating and kayak. In winter, homes within about 20 minutes of a ski area get more bookings.

## Step 5: Run the real numbers

AirDNA data for the 12 months to August 2026 put the average Pocono short-term rental at about $55,200 in revenue, with 43% occupancy and an average nightly rate of about $397. By area, averages ranged from about $45,600 around Jim Thorpe to about $62,300 around East Stroudsburg. Revenue is not profit.

Estimate the income from comparable rentals nearby, season by season, and then subtract every cost: mortgage, property taxes, insurance (including coverage for short-term rentals), POA dues, utilities, internet, cleaning, supplies, maintenance, platform fees, management if you use it, license fees and a reserve for repairs. A home that looks profitable at peak-season rates may not be once the slow months, such as March and April, are counted.

## Step 6: Financing a Poconos Airbnb

How you plan to use the home decides the loan, so tell your lender honestly.

- **Second-home loan.** Under Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac rules, a conventional second-home loan can go up to 90% of the price, so about 10% down. The home must be a single unit, you must live in it for part of the year and control it yourself, and no management company can control who stays there. Fannie Mae allows some rental income, but it cannot be used to qualify you.
- **Investment-property loan.** If the home will be a full-time rental, it is an investment property. Conventional loans go up to 85% of the price for a single-family home (about 15% down) and 75% for a 2 to 4 unit building (about 25% down).
- **Loans based on the rent.** Some lenders qualify you on the property's expected rental income instead of your own (often called DSCR loans). They usually need a larger down payment and carry higher rates.
- **FHA, VA and USDA loans** are for a home you live in as your main home, not for a vacation rental.

Talk to a licensed loan officer early, so the numbers you run are real.

## Questions

### Is the Poconos good for Airbnb?

It can be. The Poconos draw visitors from New York, New Jersey and Philadelphia all year, and AirDNA put the average Pocono short-term rental at about $55,200 in revenue in the 12 months to August 2026. Whether a home works depends on the township's rules, the community's rules, the price you pay and the full cost of running it.

### Which Monroe County townships allow short-term rentals?

In Ryth's assessment as of October 2026, the easiest are Tunkhannock, Tobyhanna, Coolbaugh, Barrett and Middle Smithfield townships. Polk, Eldred, Jackson, Stroud and Price townships allow them with limits or have unsettled rules. Ryth rates the other ten municipalities, including the boroughs of Stroudsburg and East Stroudsburg, as very difficult. Rules change, so always confirm with the township.

### Can I put any house in the Poconos on Airbnb?

No. Many townships require a permit and an inspection, allow rentals only in certain zones, or ban them in residential areas, and many communities restrict or ban them too. Always confirm both before you buy.

### How much does a Pocono vacation rental make?

According to AirDNA, the average Pocono short-term rental brought in about $55,200 in the 12 months to August 2026, and most areas averaged between about $45,000 and $65,000. Homes with standout features can earn much more, and costs come out of that revenue.

### What taxes does a Pocono short-term rental collect?

In Monroe County, stays of fewer than 30 days are generally subject to Pennsylvania's 6% hotel occupancy tax and the county's 3% hotel room excise tax. Some booking platforms collect certain taxes for you; confirm what yours covers.

### How much do I need down to buy an Airbnb in the Poconos?

Under Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac rules, about 10% for a second home that you also use and rent only some of the time, and about 15% for a single-family home bought as a full-time rental. Loans based on the rental income usually need more. Your lender confirms the exact terms.
