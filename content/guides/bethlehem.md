## Where Bethlehem is

{{map}}

Bethlehem is a city in the Lehigh Valley, between Allentown to the west and Easton to the east. Most of the city is in Northampton County, and its west side is in Lehigh County. The Lehigh River runs through the middle of town, with the historic downtown on the north side and SteelStacks on the South Side.

## Homes and communities

- Historic rowhomes, twins and Victorian homes on the North Side and the South Side
- Single-family homes in established neighborhoods
- Newer homes and townhomes in the townships around the city, such as Bethlehem Township and Hanover Township
- Multi-unit homes and rentals near the universities

## Getting around

- US Route 22 and Route 378 connect the city with Allentown and Easton, and I-78 runs along the south edge of town.
- Lehigh Valley International Airport is about 10 minutes away.
- New York City is about 80 miles away (roughly 1.5 to 2 hours), and Philadelphia about 60 miles (roughly 1 hour 15 minutes).

## What to check before you buy

- The water service line. Look up the address on the city's Service Line Inventory map, which shows what the water line to the home is made of, on both the city side and the private side, so you know before you buy whether it is lead.
- Help with the down payment. If you have not owned a home in the past three years and your household income qualifies, the city's Bethlehem Homebuyer Assistance Program can cover up to 10% of the price for the down payment, plus closing costs, as a 0% loan forgiven over 10 years. You apply once you have a signed agreement of sale for a home inside the city.
- Historic district rules. In Bethlehem's historic districts, exterior changes such as windows, doors, roofing, paint colors and fences go to the Historic and Architectural Review Board (HARB) and then City Council before the city issues a permit. If you plan to renovate the outside, check this first.
- Which county, and which municipality. The county tax depends on whether the home is on the Northampton or the Lehigh side. A "Bethlehem" mailing address can also be in Bethlehem Township or Hanover Township, which have their own taxes and rules.
- Rental licenses, if you are buying to rent. Every rental unit in the city needs a yearly license and is inspected every three years, and a unit rented to 3 to 5 unrelated people needs a separate regulated rental license.
- Older homes. Homes built before 1978 come with a lead-based paint disclosure, and older wiring, plumbing and foundations deserve a careful inspection.
- Flood zones near the Lehigh River and Monocacy Creek.
- The homestead exclusion. Once the home is your main residence, apply to the county for Pennsylvania's homestead exclusion, which lowers your school property taxes. The yearly deadline is March 1.

Selling a home in Bethlehem? The city requires its own inspection before the sale closes. See [How to sell your house in the Lehigh Valley](sell-your-house-lehigh-valley.html).

## Questions

### Is Bethlehem in Northampton County or Lehigh County?

Both. Most of the city is in Northampton County, and the west side is in Lehigh County. Property taxes depend on which side the home is on.

### Is there help for first-time buyers in Bethlehem?

Yes. The City of Bethlehem's Homebuyer Assistance Program helps income-qualified buyers who have not owned a home in the past three years, with up to 10% of the price for the down payment plus closing costs, as a 0% loan forgiven over 10 years. The home has to be inside the city, and you apply after you sign the agreement of sale. Pennsylvania's PHFA programs can also help; see the [first-time home buyer guide](first-time-home-buyer-pennsylvania.html).

### How far is Bethlehem from New York City and Philadelphia?

About 80 miles from New York City (roughly 1.5 to 2 hours by car) and about 60 miles from Philadelphia (roughly 1 hour 15 minutes).

### Is Bethlehem a good place to buy a rental property?

Bethlehem has steady rental demand, in part from its two universities, but the right property depends on your budget and goals. Ryth works with investors and can walk you through the numbers and the city's rental license rules.
