## Living in Bethlehem

Bethlehem was founded by Moravian settlers in 1741, and its historic district, with stone buildings along Main Street and Church Street, is one of the best-preserved colonial settlements in the country. The city is home to **Lehigh University** and **Moravian University**. On the South Side, the former Bethlehem Steel plant has become **SteelStacks**, an arts and concert campus, alongside the Wind Creek casino resort. Bethlehem also hosts **Musikfest** every August and is known as the "Christmas City."

## Getting around

- **US Route 22** and **Route 378** connect the city to Allentown and Easton, and **I-78** runs just south of town.
- **Lehigh Valley International Airport** is a few minutes away.
- New York City is about 80 miles away (roughly 1.5 to 2 hours), and Philadelphia about 60 miles (roughly 1 hour 15 minutes).

## Homes in and around Bethlehem

- Historic rowhomes, twins and Victorian homes on the North and South sides
- Single-family homes in established neighborhoods
- Suburban homes and newer construction in neighboring townships such as Bethlehem Township and Hanover Township
- Multi-unit homes and rental properties near the universities

## What to check before you buy

- **Which county and municipality.** Bethlehem spans two counties, and a "Bethlehem" mailing address may be in a neighboring township, so taxes and services differ.
- **Older homes.** Homes built before 1978 come with a lead-based paint disclosure, and older systems deserve a careful inspection.
- **Rental rules,** if you are buying to rent. The city has its own rental registration and inspection requirements; confirm the current rules.
- **The resale inspection** the municipality may require at sale.

## Questions

### Is Bethlehem in Northampton County or Lehigh County?

Both. Most of the city is in Northampton County, and the western part is in Lehigh County. Property taxes depend on which side the home is on.

### How far is Bethlehem from New York City and Philadelphia?

About 80 miles from New York City (roughly 1.5 to 2 hours by car) and about 60 miles from Philadelphia (roughly 1 hour 15 minutes).
