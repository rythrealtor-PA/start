## How much you can get

- A no-interest second mortgage of up to $20,000, or 20% of the purchase price, whichever is less. It can go toward the down payment, the closing costs or both.
- $5,000 more if you have lived in Monroe County for at least the past 12 months in a row.

For example, on a $200,000 home, 20% would be $40,000, so the $20,000 limit applies. A buyer who has lived in the county for the past year could get $25,000 in all.

The program is a partnership between the Monroe County Commissioners, the Monroe County Affordable Housing Board and participating mortgage lenders, paid for by the county's Affordable Housing Trust Fund.

## Who qualifies

To qualify, you must:

- Be a first-time homebuyer, as the program defines it.
- Have a household income at or below 100% of Monroe County's median income (see the limits below).
- Put in at least 3% of the purchase price from your own money.
- Buy an existing home in Monroe County. New construction does not qualify.
- Live in the home as your primary residence.
- Be a U.S. citizen or a permanent resident.
- Complete a pre-purchase homeownership counseling workshop approved by HUD.
- Qualify for a first mortgage with a participating lender, which means good credit and the ability to make the monthly payments.

## Income limits

These are the limits the Redevelopment Authority lists as of October 2026. They are set each year from HUD's median income for Monroe County, so confirm them when you apply.

| Household size | Income limit |
| --- | --- |
| 1 person | $69,750 |
| 2 people | $79,375 |
| 3 people | $89,313 |
| 4 people | $99,188 |
| 5 people | $107,125 |
| 6 people | $115,135 |
| 7 people | $123,000 |
| 8 people | $130,937 |

## How the loan is forgiven

The assistance is a second mortgage on the home with no interest and no monthly payments. Each year you live in the home, 10% of it is forgiven, so after 10 years you owe nothing.

During those 10 years, you may have to repay what is left if:

- You sell or transfer the home.
- You stop living in it as your primary residence.
- You pay off your first mortgage, or refinance it for more than the original purchase price.
- You default on either mortgage.
- The application had incomplete, false or misleading information.

## How to apply

1. Call the Redevelopment Authority of the County of Monroe at (570) 421-4300, or email info@monroecountyrda.org, to confirm you are eligible. You can apply online through the portal on its website, monroecountyrda.org. The office is at 701 Main Street, Suite 502, in Stroudsburg.
2. Complete a HUD-approved pre-purchase counseling workshop.
3. Get pre-approved for your first mortgage with a participating lender.
4. Find an existing home in Monroe County that fits the program, then make your offer.

## Questions

### Is the Monroe County program a grant?

No. It is a 0% second mortgage that is forgiven 10% a year over 10 years. If you live in the home for the full 10 years, you owe nothing; if you sell sooner, you repay the part not yet forgiven.

### How much can I get from the Monroe County First-Time Homebuyer Program?

Up to $20,000, or 20% of the purchase price if that is less, plus $5,000 more if you have lived in Monroe County for the past 12 months, for up to $25,000 in total.

### What is the income limit?

Household income must be at or below 100% of Monroe County's median income. As of October 2026, that is $69,750 for one person, $99,188 for a household of four and $130,937 for a household of eight.

### Can I use it to buy a new construction home?

No. The program is for existing homes in Monroe County that you will live in as your primary residence.

### Do I need my own money to use the program?

Yes. You have to put in at least 3% of the purchase price from your own funds.

### Who runs the program?

The Redevelopment Authority of the County of Monroe, at (570) 421-4300, in partnership with the Monroe County Commissioners, the Monroe County Affordable Housing Board and participating lenders.
