## Where Northampton County is

{{map}}

Northampton County and Lehigh County together make up the Lehigh Valley. The Delaware River is the county's eastern border with New Jersey, and the Lehigh River runs through Bethlehem and meets the Delaware at Easton. These are the roads that connect it:

- I-78 crosses the Delaware from New Jersey just south of Easton and runs west past Hellertown and Bethlehem to Allentown.
- US Route 22 crosses the river at the Easton-Phillipsburg Toll Bridge and runs through Easton and Bethlehem to Allentown.
- Route 33 runs north from I-78 past Nazareth and Wind Gap to I-80 in Monroe County.
- Route 611 follows the Delaware north from Easton to Portland. Drivers on I-80 in New Jersey can cross into Portland on the Portland-Columbia Toll Bridge.
- Lehigh Valley International Airport (ABE) is just over the line in Lehigh County.

From Bethlehem, New York City is about 1.5 to 2 hours by car and Philadelphia about 1 hour 15 minutes. Easton is about 70 miles from New York.

## Homes in Northampton County

- Historic rowhomes, twins and Victorian homes in Bethlehem and Easton
- Single-family homes in established suburban townships
- Newer construction, townhomes and planned communities, often with an HOA
- Farmland, acreage and larger homes in the northern part of the county

## Property taxes, schools and colleges

As everywhere in Pennsylvania, property taxes go to the county, the municipality and the school district, and each sets its own rate. Northampton County has several school districts, including Bethlehem Area, Easton Area, Nazareth Area, Northampton Area, Bangor Area, Pen Argyl Area, Saucon Valley and Wilson Area, so the exact address matters. Ryth gets you the actual tax bill before you make an offer.

The county is also home to Lehigh University and Moravian University in Bethlehem, Lafayette College in Easton, and Northampton Community College.

## What to check before you buy

- The municipality. A "Bethlehem" or "Easton" mailing address can be in the city or in a neighboring township, with different taxes and services.
- The resale or use and occupancy (U&O) inspection. Many municipalities here, including the City of Bethlehem, require one before a home is sold.
- Older homes. Homes built before 1978 come with a lead-based paint disclosure, and older wiring, plumbing and foundations deserve a careful inspection.
- Flood zones near the Lehigh and Delaware rivers and their creeks.
- HOA rules and fees in newer communities.

## Questions

### Is Northampton County part of the Lehigh Valley?

Yes. The Lehigh Valley is made up of Lehigh County and Northampton County.

### Does I-80 go through Northampton County?

No. I-80 enters Pennsylvania from New Jersey at the Delaware Water Gap, in Monroe County, just north of Northampton County. From Northampton County, the direct roads into New Jersey are I-78 and US Route 22, and Route 33 connects the county to I-80.

### How far is Northampton County from New York City?

Easton is about 70 miles from Manhattan, roughly 1.5 hours by car without heavy traffic. Bethlehem is about 80 miles away.

### Can Ryth help me in both the Lehigh Valley and the Poconos?

Yes. Ryth serves both Monroe County and Northampton County, which helps if you are deciding between the two. Route 33 connects them in well under an hour.
