## Where Northampton County is

Northampton County makes up the eastern half of the Lehigh Valley, together with Lehigh County to the west. The Delaware River forms its eastern border with New Jersey, and the Lehigh River runs through Bethlehem and meets the Delaware at Easton. To the north, the county rises toward the Blue Mountain ridge and the Poconos.

## Getting around

- **I-78** runs east into New Jersey and toward New York City.
- **US Route 22** connects Easton, Bethlehem and Allentown across the valley.
- **Route 33** runs north through Nazareth to I-80 and the Poconos.
- **Lehigh Valley International Airport** (ABE) is just across the county line in Lehigh County.
- Approximate drive times from Bethlehem are about 1.5 to 2 hours to New York City and about 1 hour 15 minutes to Philadelphia. Easton is the closest point to New York.

## Types of homes

- Historic rowhomes, twins and Victorian homes in Bethlehem and Easton
- Single-family homes in established suburban townships
- Newer construction, townhomes and planned communities, often with an HOA
- Farmland, acreage and luxury homes in the northern part of the county

## Property taxes

As everywhere in Pennsylvania, property taxes go to the county, the municipality and the school district, and each sets its own rate. Northampton County has several school districts, including Bethlehem Area, Easton Area, Nazareth Area and Northampton Area, so the exact address matters. Ryth will get you the actual tax bill before you make an offer.

## What to check before you buy

- **The municipality.** A "Bethlehem" or "Easton" mailing address can be in the city or in a neighboring township, with different taxes and services.
- **Use and occupancy (U&O) inspections.** Many municipalities require one when a home is sold.
- **Older homes.** Homes built before 1978 come with a lead-based paint disclosure, and older wiring, plumbing and foundations deserve a careful inspection.
- **Flood zones** near the Lehigh and Delaware rivers and their creeks.
- **HOA rules and fees** in newer communities.

## Questions

### Is Northampton County part of the Lehigh Valley?

Yes. The Lehigh Valley is made up of Lehigh County and Northampton County.

### How far is Northampton County from New York City?

Easton is about 70 miles from Manhattan, roughly 1.5 hours by car without heavy traffic. Bethlehem is about 80 miles away.

### Can Ryth help me in both the Lehigh Valley and the Poconos?

Yes. Ryth serves both Monroe County and Northampton County, which is helpful if you are deciding between the two. Route 33 connects them in well under an hour.
