## Living in Easton

Downtown Easton centers on Centre Square, with restaurants, the **Easton Public Market** and the **Crayola Experience** within walking distance. **Lafayette College** sits on College Hill above downtown. The rivers shape the city: there are riverfront parks, the canal towpaths, and views across the Delaware to New Jersey.

## Getting around

- **I-78** runs just south of the city into New Jersey, the most direct route to New York City.
- **US Route 22** connects Easton with Bethlehem and Allentown.
- New York City is about 70 miles away, roughly 1.5 hours by car without heavy traffic; Philadelphia is about 1 hour 15 minutes away.

## Homes in and around Easton

- Historic rowhomes and Victorian homes in neighborhoods such as College Hill and the West Ward
- Single-family homes in neighboring townships such as Forks, Palmer and Williams
- Newer construction and townhome communities
- Larger homes and acreage outside the city

## What to check before you buy

- **Flood zones.** Homes near the Delaware and Lehigh rivers and Bushkill Creek may be in a flood zone. If the home is in a high-risk flood zone and you have a federally backed mortgage, flood insurance is required.
- **The municipality.** An "Easton" mailing address may be in the city or a neighboring township, with different taxes and school districts.
- **Older homes.** Lead-based paint disclosure for homes built before 1978, plus a careful look at older systems.
- **The resale inspection** the municipality may require at sale.

## Questions

### How far is Easton from New York City?

About 70 miles, or roughly 1.5 hours by car via I-78. It is the closest Lehigh Valley city to New York.

### Do I need flood insurance in Easton?

Only for some homes. If the home is in a high-risk flood zone and your mortgage is federally backed, the lender will require it. Ryth checks the flood map for every home, and it is worth considering even where it is optional.

### What school district serves Easton?

The city is served by the Easton Area School District. Neighboring townships may be in other districts, so confirm the district for the exact address.
