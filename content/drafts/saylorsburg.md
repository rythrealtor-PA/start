## Living in Saylorsburg

Saylorsburg has a rural feel, with room to spread out and quick access to both the Poconos and the Lehigh Valley. Saylors Lake sits at the center of the community, and the West End's shops and services, including Ryth's own office in Brodheadsville, are a few minutes away.

## Getting around

- **Route 33** runs through Saylorsburg: north to I-80 and Stroudsburg (about 15 to 20 minutes), south to the Lehigh Valley.
- Bethlehem and Allentown are about 35 to 45 minutes away.
- New York City is about 90 miles away, roughly 1 hour 45 minutes to 2 hours by car.

## Homes in Saylorsburg

- Single-family homes on generous lots
- Homes near Saylors Lake
- Farmhouses, acreage and land for building
- Newer homes in small developments

## What to check before you buy

- **The township and school district.** Saylorsburg addresses fall in more than one township, so taxes and the school district depend on the exact address.
- **Well and septic,** since most homes here are not on public water and sewer.
- **Zoning and land use,** if you are buying acreage or land to build on.
- **Radon testing,** which is standard practice in Pennsylvania.

## Questions

### What school district serves Saylorsburg?

It depends on the address: most of the area is served by the Pleasant Valley School District, and some addresses by the Stroudsburg Area School District. Ryth confirms it for every home.

### How close is Saylorsburg to the Lehigh Valley?

Close. Route 33 takes you to Bethlehem and Allentown in about 35 to 45 minutes, which makes Saylorsburg popular with people who work in the Lehigh Valley but want more space.

### Where is Ryth's office?

At 1636 US 209, Suite 106, in Brodheadsville, PA 18322, a few minutes from Saylorsburg.
