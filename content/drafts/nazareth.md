## Living in Nazareth

Nazareth was founded by Moravian settlers in the 1740s, and its historic buildings still define the downtown around Center Square. **C. F. Martin & Co.** has made guitars here for generations, and its museum and factory tours attract visitors from around the world. The surrounding townships mix established neighborhoods, newer developments and farmland.

## Getting around

- **Route 33** runs just west of town: south to I-78 and US Route 22, north to I-80 and the Poconos.
- **Route 248** connects Nazareth with Easton and the northern Lehigh Valley.
- Bethlehem is about 15 minutes away, Easton about 20, and Stroudsburg about 35.
- New York City is about 80 miles away, roughly 1.5 to 2 hours by car.

## Homes in and around Nazareth

- Historic homes in the borough
- Single-family homes in Upper Nazareth, Lower Nazareth and Bushkill townships
- Newer construction and planned communities
- Larger lots and acreage farther out

## What to check before you buy

- **The municipality.** A "Nazareth" mailing address often means one of the surrounding townships, not the borough itself, with different taxes and services.
- **HOA rules and fees** in newer communities.
- **Well and septic** for homes outside the borough.
- **The resale inspection** the municipality may require at sale.

## Questions

### Is Nazareth close to the Poconos?

Yes. Route 33 runs north from Nazareth to I-80, and Stroudsburg is about 35 minutes away, so Nazareth works well for people who want the Lehigh Valley with the mountains nearby.

### What school district serves Nazareth?

The Nazareth Area School District serves the borough and several surrounding townships. Confirm the district for the exact address.

### Does a Nazareth address mean the home is in the borough?

Not necessarily. Many Nazareth mailing addresses are in neighboring townships, so Ryth confirms the municipality, taxes and school district for every home.
