# Planazo · Elevator pitch (vertical 1080×1920) · Treatment

**Goal:** a 1:20–1:45 pitch where the viewer *sees the web app working*. The founder records the voice-over separately, so the picture leaves room and time for each phrase.
**Material:** three real iPhone screen recordings of the app (1180×2556, 60 fps), the Planazo logo (taken from the app header), no character animation.

## Three possible structures

1. **Phone on a brand background, with "point and show"** (chosen). The real recording is always inside a phone, and the camera moves into the area that matters. A digital cursor shows where to tap and scroll, orange circles and labels mark what the voice is saying, and short texts above support each phrase. The website is the protagonist from the first to the last second.
2. **Full-screen recording, no phone.** Maximum UI size, but it reads as a plain screen recording, there's no space for texts without covering the app, and it loses the "product" feel.
3. **Split screen (text left / phone right).** Elegant in landscape, but at 1080 px wide it shrinks the phone too much and the app can't be read.

**Why 1:** it keeps the recording's quality (camera zooms of up to 1.5× still use the original pixels), turns every phrase of the script into a concrete visual action in the app, and fits 9:16.

## Look
- Cream background from the app (#FFF6EC) with a soft peach glow and faint sparkles, a nod to the logo's ✦.
- Graphite phone with a soft shadow. The recording runs inside it, with its real Dynamic Island.
- Text in Manrope 800 (close to the app's headlines), black #131110 with key words in Planazo orange #FF5F00.
- "Point and show": hand-drawn orange circles, highlight boxes, dark pills with labels, and a white arrow cursor with an orange ripple on each tap and a trail when it swipes. Every tap is synced to the recording's real frame (±1/60 s).

## Arc
Problem (screen dimmed → "recharges" with the battery) → brand (logo + header circled) → indecision (the real dropdown changing 4 times) → solution (app web, no downloads, browser, filters, "Generar mi plan", itinerary) → audience (results with photos, group plan saved) → Freemium (Free S/0 → Plus S/9.90 → features highlighted one by one) → closing headline → CTA with the logo and the photo of friends.

## Sound
Original background music, about 100 BPM, warm and light. It starts quiet and "tired", lifts on the logo, hesitates during the indecision, settles into a light groove during the demo, opens up on the headline and closes on a final chord at "¡Muchas gracias!". It sits low for the voice, and there are very soft clicks on each tap.
