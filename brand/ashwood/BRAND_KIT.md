## Required on every post (PA advertising + brand rules)

1. Brokerage name **exactly as registered** with the PA Real Estate Commission, plus the office phone **(570) 534-4404**.
2. Brokerage name at least as prominent as the team name (Heather Schierloh Collective) — brokers confirm final rule.
3. Equal Housing Opportunity logo or text on listing and service posts.
4. Fair Housing: describe the property and area, never the people. Avoid "perfect for families", "exclusive", "young professionals".
5. No "#1" or "best agent" claims. Use the exact award name: "Community's Choice Award — Real Estate Agent, 2026".
6. Every number (years, homes sold, prices, stats) true and sourced.
7. Testimonials: real, with the client's permission, first names only.
8. Never advertise another brokerage's listing without written permission; never criticize other agents or brokerages (including the brokers' previous brokerage).
9. Tax/loan content: general information only — "talk to your CPA/lender".

## Voice

Warm, confident, local, plain-spoken. Experts who explain how things work.
Fewer emojis, no hype ("DM me 🔥🔥"). English first; Spanish versions welcome ("Hablamos español").

### Seasonal palette: autumn (Fact of the Day posts)

Deep chestnut `#2B120A` (footer band, text cards), gold `#F4C35A` (kickers, italic lines), cream `#FFF4E0` (headlines), peach `#F3D9B8` (body/footer text). Backgrounds are generated by `posts/fact-of-the-day-ashwood-tree/backgrounds/gen.py` (SVG) and rendered with `render.js` (Playwright, 2x).

### Palette: night forest (Build Your Cabin)

Forest green `#0E2A20` (cards, 55% opacity) and `#0A1D17` (footer band), gold `#FFC56B` (A/B labels, italic lines), cream `#F3E6CC` (headlines, body). Art from `posts/build-your-cabin/art/cabin.py`.

### Palette: vintage postcard (1950s vs Today)

Paper `#EFDDBA`, navy `#1F2A44` (headlines, footer band), coral `#D9583B` (kickers, "TODAY"), teal `#2C7A7B`, mustard `#E8B04A`. Uses the navy logo. Art from `posts/1950s-vs-today/art/retro.py`.

Facts used (checked): the Delaware Water Gap Toll Bridge opened Dec 1953 and now carries I-80; after WWII the Poconos became a top honeymoon destination. AI images must stay labeled "AI-generated illustrations" and must not show real business names (the 1950s resort image is cropped to hide an AI-made "Pocono Manor" sign).
