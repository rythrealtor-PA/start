## Fact check (phfa.org, checked Nov 24, 2026)

| Slide | On the slide | What PHFA says |
|---|---|---|
| K-FIT | 5% of the price; forgiven 10% a year over 10 years | "5% of the lesser of the purchase price or appraised value with no maximum dollar limit"; second mortgage "forgiven on an annual basis over ten years at a rate of ten percent (10%) a year". Paired with Keystone Flex/Keystone Home Loan |
| K-DATE | 5% (8% on loans up to $150K); 0% interest, no monthly payment, due when you sell or refinance | Loans ≤ $150,000: 8% (subject to availability); ≥ $150,001: 5%. 0% interest, 30-year term, no monthly payments, due at sale, refinance or payoff |
| Keystone Advantage | Up to $6,000 or 4%, whichever is less; 0% interest, paid back over 10 years | "Up to four percent (4%) of the purchase price or market value or $6,000 (whichever is less)"; second mortgage "amortized over a ten year term at zero percent (0%) interest" |
| Buy + Fix | Keystone Flex Purchase & Improvement: up to $30,000 | Conventional first mortgage "with funds up to $30,000 to make eligible repairs and improvements", paired with K-FIT or K-DATE |
| Who qualifies | 660+ credit score for the assistance programs; income and price limits vary by county | All 3 assistance programs: "Minimum credit score of 660". Income and purchase price limits by county (PHFA Seller's Guide appendices A and F) |
| First-time buyer | No home owned in the past 3 years = first-time; veterans and some others qualify either way | Keystone Home Loan: first-time buyer = "has not owned (had Title to) their principal residence during the previous three years"; discharged veterans and buyers in targeted areas qualify even if not first-time. Keystone Flex and HFA Preferred have no first-time buyer requirement |
| Take the class | Homebuyer education is required for many programs, through a PHFA-approved counseling agency | Keystone Flex: "All borrowers must complete homebuyer education from an approved PHFA Counseling Agency". Others require it below a 680 score |

- The 3 assistance programs can't be combined with each other (PHFA: "May not be combined with any other PHFA assistance program", except the Access Home Modification program). This is on slide 9.
- Other limits not on the slides: liquid assets can't be over $50,000 after closing, and the minimum assistance loan is $500. Mention them in DMs.
- Sources: https://www.phfa.org/programs/assistance.aspx and https://www.phfa.org/programs/homepurchase.aspx
