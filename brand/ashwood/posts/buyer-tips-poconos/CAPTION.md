## Fact check
- **Radon:** PA DEP says about 40% of Pennsylvania homes test above the EPA action level of 4 pCi/L (same source as your seller post: pa.gov DEP radon news releases, Jan 2025 and Jan 2026). The source line is on the slide.
- **Short-term rentals:** many Monroe County townships require a short-term rental permit or registration (for example Pocono, Stroud, Middle Smithfield and Paradise Townships), and many HOAs ban or limit rentals. The slide says "many townships", not "all".
- **Wells, septic, private roads, oil/propane heat and internet:** general Poconos buying tips with no numbers. Check the listing details for each home.

## Caption (EN)

Buying in the Poconos? 7 things nobody tells you before you buy 🔑👇
1️⃣ Well & septic: many homes aren't on town water or sewer. Test the water and inspect the septic before closing
2️⃣ Private roads: owners or the HOA may pay for plowing. Ask who plows and what the road fee is
3️⃣ HOA rules: read them before you sign (rentals, sheds, fences)
4️⃣ Internet: it can change street by street. Check the exact address with providers
5️⃣ Planning an Airbnb? Many townships require a permit. Check the township AND the HOA
6️⃣ Oil or propane heat: ask the seller for last year's heating bills
7️⃣ Radon: about 40% of PA homes test above the EPA action level (PA DEP). Add a radon test to your inspection
