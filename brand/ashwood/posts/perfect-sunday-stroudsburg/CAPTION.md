## The plan (each stop about 10 minutes from the next)

| Time | Stop | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 🥂 Sunday brunch at **Stroudsmoor Country Inn**, then 🛍️ a stroll down Main Street | Stroudsmoor Rd and Main St, Stroudsburg |
| Afternoon | 🥾 Hike **Mount Minsi** on the Appalachian Trail, with views from 🏞️ **Lookout Rock** | Lake Lenape trailhead, Mountain Rd, Delaware Water Gap |
| Night | 🎷 Live jazz at the **Deer Head Inn** | 5 Main St, Delaware Water Gap |

## Check before posting

- **Stroudsmoor:** known for its Sunday brunch. Confirm this Sunday's hours and whether you need a reservation.
- **Mount Minsi:** about a 4.7–5 mile loop, moderate and rocky, with about 20 parking spots and no restrooms. Wear real boots in winter.
- **Deer Head Inn:** music listed Wednesday or Thursday through Sunday; the Sunday lineup changes. Check deerheadinn.com, or call 570-424-2000 for reservations. There's a music charge at the door.
