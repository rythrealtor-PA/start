| Time | Plan | Where |

| Friday | 🛍️ Black Friday at **The Crossings Premium Outlets** | 1000 Route 611, Tannersville, off I-80 |

## Check before posting

- **Turkey Trot:** listed for Thursday, Nov 26, 2026. One listing gives the start at 8:00 AM, the same time registration opens, so confirm the start time with Skytop (570-595-8998, last year's number).
- **Kalahari buffet:** the 2026 Thanksgiving buffet is on sale through Tock; reservations required.
- **The Crossings:** 2026 Black Friday hours aren't published yet. Last year the Nike store there was closed Thanksgiving Day and opened at 6 AM Friday; other stores may differ. Check premiumoutlets.com before posting hours.
