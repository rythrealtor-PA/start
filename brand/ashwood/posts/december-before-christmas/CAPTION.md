## Check before posting

- **Olde Time Christmas (Jim Thorpe):** the 2026 listing gives Nov 6 – Dec 20 with weekend carolers. Last year's main weekends were Dec 6–7, 13–14 and 20–21. Confirm on jimthorpe.org.
- **Quiet Valley:** 36th annual event. Tickets in advance are $16 for adults and $8 for kids 3–12 ($18 / $10 at the gate). Tours run every 15 minutes in the afternoon. One listing gave different second-weekend dates, so confirm at quietvalley.org or 570-992-6161.
- **Hawley Winterfest parade:** listed for Saturday, Dec 12, 2026, 11 AM. The rest of the 2026 Winterfest schedule wasn't published yet.
- **Ski opening:** Camelback and Blue Mountain haven't announced 2026 opening dates (Camelback opened Dec 3 last season). Check before posting, or change slide 5 to "Opening soon".
