## The plan (every stop within 10 minutes of the next)

| Time | Stop | Where | Drive to next |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | 🍳 Brunch at **The Morning Toast at Barley Creek** | 1774 Sullivan Trail, Tannersville | ~10 min |
| Afternoon | ⛷️ Ski or snow tube at **Camelback Mountain** (or 🛍️ **The Crossings Premium Outlets**) | Camelback Rd / Route 611, Tannersville | ~10 min |
| Night | 🥩 Dinner at the **Legendary Tannersville Inn** | Route 611, Tannersville | — |

Fun facts on the slides:
- Barley Creek opened in 1995 as the first microbrewery in the Poconos since Prohibition.
- The Tannersville Inn has been "a Pocono tradition since 1825."

## Check before posting

- **Brunch:** The Morning Toast serves brunch Saturday and Sunday, about 9 AM–2 PM. Barley Creek: 570-629-9399.
- **Camelback:** the 2026/27 winter opening date hasn't been announced yet (last season opened Dec 3), and snow-tubing status varies. Check camelbackresort.com or call 855-515-1283. Before snow season, swap the afternoon to the Aquatopia indoor waterpark or the outlets.
- **Tannersville Inn:** listings show 2977 PA-611, phone (570) 629-3131. One township page lists another address, so confirm it.
