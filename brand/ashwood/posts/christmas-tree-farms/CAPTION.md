## Fact check (verified Nov 19–24, 2026)

| Farm | On the slide | Source |
|---|---|---|
| DeHaven's Christmas Tree Farm | 6 kinds: Douglas, Fraser, balsam, Canaan and concolor firs, plus Colorado blue spruce. Wagon rides (when weather permits) and handmade wreaths. Effort Neola Rd, Brodheadsville. Open Fri–Sun Nov 27–29, then Sat/Sun Dec 5–6, 12–13 and 19–20, 9:30 AM–4 PM. (570) 992-5182 | dehavenschristmastrees.com, discovernepa.com |
| Yenser's Tree Farm | 2026 prices start at $45 (under 6 ft). Free cocoa and cookies on weekends. 1910 Mahoning Drive East, Lehighton. Open Nov 21–22, then daily Nov 27 – Dec 6. Weekends 9 AM–5 PM, weekdays 3–5 PM. (570) 249-7775 | Yenser's website (2026 season page) |
| Heckman Orchards | Pick-your-own Christmas trees, hand saws available, wreaths. Effort, PA. "Generally every weekend in December until we close." (570) 629-1191 | heckmanorchards.com/pick-your-own, DiscoverNEPA |

- DiscoverNEPA lists DeHaven's as Saylorsburg; the farm's own site says Brodheadsville. The slide uses Brodheadsville.
- Heckman's season depends on stock, so the slide says "Call ahead."
- Yenser's full 2026 price list: under 6' $45, 6–7' $55, 7–8' $65, 8–9' $80, 9–10' $95, 10–11' $110, 11–12' $125. They also shake, bale, drill and tie trees to your car for free.
