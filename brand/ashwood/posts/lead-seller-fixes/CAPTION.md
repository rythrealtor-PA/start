## Fact check

**HOA certificate (slide 7):**
- Pennsylvania's Uniform Planned Community Act, 68 Pa.C.S. § 5407, covers resale certificates.
- The buyer isn't obligated to buy until the certificate is provided, and for 5 days after, or until closing, whichever comes first.
- The slide has a source line and "General information, not legal advice."
- Condominiums have a parallel rule (68 Pa.C.S. § 3407). Check it before you say it applies to condos too.

**Radon (slide 8):**
- PA DEP says about 40% of Pennsylvania homes are above the EPA action level of 4 pCi/L.
- PA DEP gives mitigation costs of about $800–$1,200.
- Sources:
  - https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dep/newsroom/2026-01-12-dep-encourages-pennsylvanians-to-test-their-homes-for-radon-during-radon-action-month
  - https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dep/newsroom/2025-01-09-dep-encourages-pennsylvanians-to-test-their-homes-for-radon.html

**3 to skip (slide 9):** Cost vs. Value Report 2025 (Zonda / JLC), U.S. national averages, share of cost recouped at resale.

| Project | Cost recouped |
|---|---|
| Major kitchen remodel, midrange | 51% |

| Primary suite addition, midrange | 32% |
| Minor kitchen remodel, midrange | 113% |

- No 2026 edition was published yet.
- The national averages are on the slide. Results vary by home and market, and the slide says so.
- Source: https://www.jlconline.com/cost-vs-value/2025/
