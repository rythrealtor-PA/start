## Before you post (important)

1. **Get permission for each listing.** All 8 homes are other brokerages' listings. BRAND_KIT rule 8 says never advertise another brokerage's listing without written permission.
   - Text or email each listing agent (template below) and keep the replies.
   - If a brokerage says no, swap that home for one with permission, or for one of your own listings.
   - The "Listing courtesy of…" line is already on every price slide.
2. **The photo frames are empty on purpose.** They say "Listing photo (with the listing broker's permission)".
   - Listing photos belong to the brokerage or photographer, so only add them after the brokerage says yes.
   - In Canva, drag the photo onto the frame.
3. **Check every home is still active on the morning you post.** Prices and status change fast.
   - If one went under contract, swap it for another active home in the same price band.
   - Update the price slide and the "Info as of" date.
4. **Have your HOMES reply ready.** Answer every comment with a DM: "Hi! Here's the list for [budget]: …"
   - Send current MLS results from your search portal for their budget and town.
   - Or use ManyChat to auto-send a form that asks for their budget.
