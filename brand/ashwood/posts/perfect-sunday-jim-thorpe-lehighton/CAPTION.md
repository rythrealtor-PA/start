## Check before posting

- **Broadway Grille:** known for breakfast and brunch; (570) 732-4343.
- **Wild Creek Falls:** the trails are open year-round. Under 10 minutes on foot from the Wild Creek trailhead to the falls; the full loop is about 1 mile. It's rocky, so wear real shoes in winter.
  - Nearby parking closes Friday evening to Monday morning from Memorial Day to Labor Day, which doesn't matter in winter.
- **Moya:** fine dining; Sunday 5–8 PM. Book ahead.
- **Molly Maguire's:** pub and steakhouse in Jim Thorpe's oldest commercial building still in use.
- **Not on the list on purpose:** the Lehigh Gorge Scenic Railway. A rockslide on Route 209 (Oct 31) moved its trips to Nesquehoning, so check before mentioning the train in a post.
