## Check before posting

- **Broadway Grille:** known for breakfast and brunch; (570) 732-4343.
- **Wild Creek Falls:** the trails are open year-round. Under 10 minutes on foot from the Wild Creek trailhead to the falls; the full loop is about 1 mile. It's rocky, so wear real shoes in winter.
  - Nearby parking closes Friday evening to Monday morning from Memorial Day to Labor Day, which doesn't matter in winter.
- **Night slide photos don't match the restaurant:** the slide says Roadies, but its background and both frame photos show **Bonnie & Clyde Pub and Grill** (the Bonnie & Clyde doors, the "36 beers on tap" sign), at 111 N 1st St in Lehighton. Roadies is a different place, up at Penn's Peak.
  - Either change the text to Bonnie & Clyde Pub and Grill, or swap in photos of Roadies.
  - Bonnie & Clyde fits the plan too, since it's in Lehighton.
- **Roadies Restaurant and Bar:** at Penn's Peak, 325 Maury Road, Jim Thorpe, (610) 826-9100. Known for mountaintop views from two big outdoor decks. Check its winter hours before posting.
- **Not on the list on purpose:** the Lehigh Gorge Scenic Railway. A rockslide on Route 209 (Oct 31) moved its trips to Nesquehoning, so check before mentioning the train in a post.
