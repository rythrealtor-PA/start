## The plan (each stop about 5–20 minutes from the next)

| Time | Stop | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 🥐 Breakfast at **Fork Street Bistro & Bakery** | 16 Fork St, Mount Pocono |
| Afternoon | 🌴 **Kalahari's indoor waterpark**, or 🌲 a lake walk at **Tobyhanna State Park** | Pocono Manor (~5 min) / Tobyhanna (~20 min) |
| Night | 🥩 Dinner at **Bistecca by Il Mulino** | Mount Airy Casino Resort, Mount Pocono |

## Check before posting

- **Fork Street Bistro:** delivery listings show Sunday 8 AM–1 PM. There's no official hours page, so call ahead.
- **Kalahari:** waterpark day passes sell out on busy weekends. Check kalahariresorts.com for availability.
- **Tobyhanna State Park:** 170-acre lake with a beach. In winter the lake is a walk, not a swim.
- **Bistecca by Il Mulino:** "Very Expensive" on OpenTable, rated 4.7 from about 2,400 reviews. Book ahead.
- **Not on the list on purpose:** Chops Steakhouse on the Mountain is permanently closed.
