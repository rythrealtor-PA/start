## Fact check

This post is general advice with no prices, rates or program numbers.

- "Property taxes vary by township": PA property taxes are set by county, municipality and school district, so the same price can carry a different tax bill a few miles apart.
- "Well & septic, heating oil or propane": many Poconos homes are not on public water, sewer or gas. Check the listing and the seller's disclosure.
- "Sellers take pre-approved buyers more seriously": general practice, no number claimed.
- The caption ends with "General information, not financial or legal advice."
