## Fact check (Nov 2026; confirm hours before posting)

- **The Cure Cafe**, 517 Main St, Stroudsburg. Breakfast/brunch spot; weekend brunch; closed Tuesdays; (570) 664-2888. Listings disagree on Sunday hours, so call to confirm.
  - https://poconogo.com/?p=5523
  - https://discovernepa.com/where-to-eat/dining/nepa-brunch-spots/
- **Mount Minsi**: starts at Lake Lenape (Mountain Rd, Delaware Water Gap, PA) on the white-blazed Appalachian Trail. About a 4.7–5 mile loop with Lookout Rock views across to Mount Tammany. Moderate and rocky. About 20 parking spots, no restrooms.
  - https://trailconference.org/node/232312
  - https://myhikes.org/trails/mt-minsi-via-appalachian-trail
- **Deer Head Inn**, 5 Main St, Delaware Water Gap. Jazz club with music Wednesday–Sunday and dinner Thursday–Sunday. Sunday doors listed at 4 PM. Music charge at the door; reservations at 570-424-2000. Check the monthly schedule at deerheadinn.com.
  - https://discovernepa.com/listing/the-deer-head-inn/
  - https://www.pikecountycourier.com/entertainment/the-longest-running-jazz-club-is-a-stones-throw-away-KQPC20140305140309993
