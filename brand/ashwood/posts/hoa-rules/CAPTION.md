## Before you post

1. **Add 2 photos per community.** Drag a photo onto each "ADD HOA PHOTO" box: the lake, beach or pool on the left, the entrance or clubhouse on the right. Use your own photos, or photos the HOA has given you permission to use. Don't copy images from HOA websites or other agents' listings.
2. **Check the numbers.** Every slide says "Summarized Nov 2026". Fees and rules change, so recheck with each association before you post, and again if you reuse the carousel later.

## What each slide says (summarized from the sources below)

**Emerald Lakes** (Long Pond · Tunkhannock Twp · Emerald Lakes Association)
- HOA: annual dues for all owners; association ID needed at the amenities.
- Amenities: lakes and beaches, indoor and outdoor pools, community center, tennis, basketball, dog park. Electric boat motors only.
- Parking: no parking on community roads, ever. One registered RV or trailer allowed on a lot with a house.
- Pets: max 2 dogs or cats per home. Leash outside your lot. Not allowed at beaches, pools, courts or the community center.
- STR: allowed. Register every rental with the Association (STR fees apply) and get a Tunkhannock Township STR permit.

**Pocono Pines** (Lake Naomi · Tobyhanna Twp · Pocono Pines Community Association)
- HOA: PPCA covers Lake Naomi. HOA dues are separate from the optional Lake Naomi Club membership.
- Amenities: Lake Naomi Club has the lake, beaches and pools. You need a club membership to use them; it isn't included in HOA dues.
- Parking: no parking on community roads without the Chief Ranger's approval. Vehicles may be towed.
- Pets: dogs on a leash under 8 ft off your property. Pick up after them. Constant barking counts as a noise violation.
- STR: allowed with a Tobyhanna Twp permit ($600 the first year). Renters get amenity access for up to 6 weeks a year, Saturday to Saturday.

**Penn Estates** (East Stroudsburg · Stroud Twp · Penn Estates POA)
- HOA: gated. The 2026 annual assessment is $1,466 per improved lot ($1,516 in 2027).
- Amenities: 3 lakes and beaches (paddle and sail only), pool, community center, fitness center, tennis, basketball, dog park.
- Parking: driveway only. No street or easement parking without a written pass. No commercial vehicles.
- Pets: dogs must be registered with the POA. Leash off your lot. No pets at lakes, pools, beaches or playgrounds.
- STR: allowed with a Stroud Twp license, $175/yr owner registration and $40 per rental. STR guests can't use the fitness center.

**Indian Mountain Lake** (Albrightsville · Indian Mountain Lake Civic Association)
- HOA: gated, 24/7 security patrol, 3,262 lots across 4 townships. Dues cover roads, trash and amenities.
- Amenities: 5 lakes and beaches, 2 pools plus a kiddie pool, clubhouse, 4 tennis and 2 basketball courts. Paddle and sail only.
- Parking: park on your own lot, off the road and shoulders. 30 minutes max at the entrance and mailboxes. RVs are OK beside or behind the house.
- Pets: leash off your property. No pets at pools or courts. Beaches only Nov 1 to Mar 31.
- STR: allowed with a $100/yr IML permit, a township STR permit and a septic inspection, plus a $60 registration per stay.

**Towamensing Trails** (Albrightsville · Penn Forest Twp · Towamensing Trails POA)
- HOA: gated lake community. Owners and renters wear wristbands at the amenities.
- Amenities: lake and beach with marina, outdoor pool, community center, courts, playground. No gas motors on the lake.
- Parking: no roadside parking; designated areas only. Campers can't be used as living quarters.
- Pets: keep pets on your property or leashed. Not allowed on the beach, lake, dam or pool area.
- STR: allowed. Renter wristbands come from the POA ($600/yr per the 2022 rules, max 8), plus a Penn Forest Twp STR permit.
