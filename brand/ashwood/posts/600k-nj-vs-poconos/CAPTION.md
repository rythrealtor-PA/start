## Before you post

1. **Re-check all 4 listings the day you post.** Prices and status change fast. Nutley was already under contract when researched. If it closes or comes off the market, swap in another NJ home near $600K and update the receipt values in Canva.
2. **Listing permission (PA homes).** 49 Seneca Rd is listed by Meisse Real Estate and 539 Tournament Ct by Real of Pennsylvania – Brodheadsville. Neither is an Ashwood listing.
   - Under PA advertising rules, you need the listing broker's written permission before you advertise another brokerage's listing.
   - Listing photos belong to the listing agent or photographer. Use them only with that permission.
   - Otherwise, use your own exterior photos taken from the street, or swap in Ashwood listings.
   - Keep the "Listing courtesy of…" line on each PA slide.
3. **NJ homes are shown as data only** (town, address, specs). No photos. Keep it that way unless you get permission from those brokerages too.
4. **Photo boxes:** on slides 3 and 5, drag a photo onto each "ADD HOME PHOTO" box: the front of the house on the left, the best interior or view on the right.
