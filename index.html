Discover Pennsylvania real estate with Ryth Vara

Ryth Vara is a bilingual REALTOR® with Real of Pennsylvania, serving Monroe County and Northampton County, including Stroudsburg, Tannersville, Pocono Pines, Saylorsburg, Bethlehem, Nazareth, Easton, and Bath.

He specializes in luxury real estate, real estate investors, and Spanish-speaking families, combining local market knowledge with modern marketing strategies.

In 2025, Ryth and his team at HS Group closed 149 transactions totaling $49 million in sales.

Ryth uses professional photography, social media, targeted advertising, multiple MLS exposure, and AI-powered property visualizations to market homes effectively.

Known for his honesty and competitive approach, he is committed to protecting his clients’ interests and getting the deal done.

He got into real estate for two reasons: to help people reach their goals with the peace of mind of knowing they are represented by the best — and because real estate is the best way to build wealth, in the short term and the long term. What drives him every day is a hunger for success and for getting every client the best possible result.
