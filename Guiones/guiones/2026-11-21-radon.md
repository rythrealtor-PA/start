There's an invisible gas in a lot of Pennsylvania homes, and most buyers never test for it.

[PUNTO 1]

[PUNTO 2]
Pennsylvania has some of the highest radon levels in the country, and the EPA calls it the second leading cause of lung cancer.

[PUNTO 3]
But here's the good news. A radon test is a small add-on to your home inspection. And if the level is high, it can usually be fixed with a mitigation system.
