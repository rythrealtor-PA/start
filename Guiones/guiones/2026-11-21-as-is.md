## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
"Sold as-is" doesn't mean what most buyers think.

[PUNTO 1]
It means the seller doesn't plan to make repairs. That's it.

[PUNTO 2]
In most cases, you can still get a home inspection. And depending on your contract, you may still be able to walk away.

[PUNTO 3]
But here's the part people get wrong. In Pennsylvania, selling as-is usually doesn't erase the seller's duty to disclose known problems.

[CIERRE]
Buying as-is? Inspect anyway. Have you ever bought a house as-is? Tell me how it went.
