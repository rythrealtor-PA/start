# No compres ese sofá todavía
| Plataforma | Duración | Objetivo | Audiencia | Formato | Modo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels/TikTok | <45 s | Viral educativo | Compradores bajo contrato | Banderas rojas | Palabra por palabra |

## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
Your house is under contract. Do not buy that couch yet.

[PUNTO 1]
Lenders can check your credit again right before closing. A new credit card or a car loan can change your approval.

[PUNTO 2]
Two: don't move big money around without a paper trail. Large deposits have to be explained.

[PUNTO 3]
And the one that surprises people the most. Three: don't change jobs before closing without talking to your lender first.

[CIERRE]
Couch after closing. Keys first. Tag someone who's about to close.

## Notas
- Texto en pantalla: `Under contract? DON'T buy the couch 🛋️`
- B-roll: tienda de muebles, alguien con la tarjeta en la mano, llaves.
- Ideal para grabar con un lender invitado (más autoridad y alcance compartido).
