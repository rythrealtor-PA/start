## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
If you own a second home in the Poconos, these three winter mistakes can cost you thousands.

[PUNTO 1]
One: turning the heat off when you leave. Pipes freeze, they burst, and you find out weeks later. Keep the heat on.

[PUNTO 2]
Two: forgetting the vents. Snow can block your furnace and dryer vents, and that's a carbon monoxide risk. Keep them clear.

[PUNTO 3]
And three: ignoring the roof. Heavy snow and ice dams can push water right into your ceiling.

[CIERRE]
Send this to someone with a place in the Poconos. They'll thank you in January.

## Notas
- Texto en pantalla: `3 winter mistakes Poconos homeowners make 🥶`
