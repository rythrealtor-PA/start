# Listo para comprar vs. poder comprar
| Plataforma | Duración | Objetivo | Audiencia | Formato | Modo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels/TikTok | <45 s | Leads compradores | Compradores primerizos | Historia real + lección | Palabra por palabra |

## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
They told me their budget was three hundred fifty thousand. The lender approved them for two hundred.

[PUNTO 1]
This happens more than you think. Being ready to buy a house and being able to buy one are two different things.

[PUNTO 2]
Your budget is what you feel comfortable paying. Your approval is what the bank says, based on your income, your debts, your credit and your down payment.

[PUNTO 3]
So before you fall in love with a house, talk to a lender. It takes one conversation and it saves you a broken heart.

[CIERRE]
Have you been pre-approved yet? Comment READY and I'll send you what to bring to your first lender meeting.

## Notas
- Historia: clientes anonimizados, sin nombres ni detalles que los identifiquen.
- Texto en pantalla: `Budget: $350K 😬 Approved: $200K`
- Sin tasas ni pagos mensuales (reglas de publicidad de crédito).
