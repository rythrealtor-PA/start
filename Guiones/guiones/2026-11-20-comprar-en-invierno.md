# Comprar en invierno
| Plataforma | Duración | Objetivo | Audiencia | Formato | Modo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels/TikTok | <45 s | Leads compradores | Compradores que piensan esperar a la primavera | Contradicción | Palabra por palabra |

## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
Everyone waits for spring to buy in the Poconos. That's exactly why you shouldn't.

[PUNTO 1]
One: less competition. Fewer buyers out in the snow means more room to negotiate.

[PUNTO 2]
Two: sellers who list in winter are often more motivated. That matters at the table.

[PUNTO 3]
But the best reason isn't the price. You see the house at its worst. The icy driveway. The heat in January. If you love it now, you'll love it in July.

[CIERRE]
Buying this winter? Comment WINTER and I'll send you what to check at a cold-weather showing.
