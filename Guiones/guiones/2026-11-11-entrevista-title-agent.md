# Entrevista: agente de title insurance → serie de reels

**Formato:** "I asked a title agent…" / "Ask the expert". Cada respuesta = 1 reel de 20–45 s.
**Objetivo:** marca + autoridad (Ashwood se rodea de expertos). **Audiencia:** compradores de los Poconos.

## Antes de grabar (2 minutos)
- Pídele permiso para usar el video en redes, y que confirme si su empresa necesita aprobarlo.
- No le pidas pagar nada de tu marketing (RESPA: co-marketing con title companies).
- Nada de nombres, direcciones ni datos de clientes en sus historias.
- Un micrófono por persona; 2 teléfonos (plano de ella + plano amplio) o 1 en horizontal.
- Que **repita la pregunta al responder** ("The scariest thing I've found in a title search was…"), así cada clip se sostiene solo.
- Que responda con **la conclusión primero**, en 20–40 s. Si se alarga: "Give me that in one sentence."

## Preguntas (y el hook del reel que sale de cada una)
| # | Pregunta | Hook del reel |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What's the scariest thing you've ever found in a title search? | "I asked a title agent the scariest thing she's ever found on a house." |
| 2 | Tell me about a closing that almost fell apart because of title, and how you saved it. | "This house almost didn't close. Here's what the title agent found." |
| 3 | Can someone sell my house without me knowing? (deed fraud / seller impersonation) | "Someone could sell your house without you knowing. I asked a title agent how." |
| 4 | How do people lose their down payment to wire fraud, and how do I protect myself? | "People are losing their entire down payment with one email." |
| 5 | Do I really need owner's title insurance if the lender already requires a policy? | "Your lender's title policy doesn't protect you. Wait, what?" |
| 6 | Is title insurance a monthly payment? How does it work? | "Most buyers think title insurance is monthly. It's not." |
| 7 | What does title insurance actually protect me from? Give me a real example. | "What title insurance actually protects you from, explained by a title agent." |
| 8 | What title issues are special to the Poconos? (HOA liens, private roads, easements, old land records) | "Buying in the Poconos? A title agent says watch out for this." |
| 9 | What's the #1 thing buyers do right before closing that causes problems? | "Don't do this the week before your closing." |
| 10 | Rapid fire, 5 seconds each: lien, easement, escrow, closing disclosure, deed. | "Real estate words explained in 5 seconds each." |
| 11 | What's one thing you wish every buyer knew before closing? | "A title agent's #1 advice before you buy a house." |
| 12 | Weirdest or funniest thing that's happened at a closing? | "The weirdest thing that ever happened at a closing." |

## Al final de la sesión
- Graba al broker solo diciendo las intros: "I asked a title agent [X]…" (5–8 s cada una), para ponerlas antes de cada respuesta.
- B-roll: manos firmando, documentos, llaves, apretón de manos, la oficina.
- Agradecimiento a cámara de ella (para el cierre de la serie).

## Edición (editar-reels)
- Un proyecto por pregunta; corte con `cut_silences.py`, vertical con `vertical.py` (crop a quien habla).
- Subtítulos con palabras clave: fraud, title, closing, lien, Poconos.
- Texto en pantalla con su nombre y cargo los primeros 3 s; cierre de marca Ashwood.
- Verifica con ella cualquier dato técnico antes de publicar; no agregues cifras que ella no dijo.
