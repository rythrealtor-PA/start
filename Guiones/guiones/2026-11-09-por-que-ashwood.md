## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
Most brokerages are named after their owner. We named ours after a tree.

[PUNTO 1]
Ashwood. Ash is the tree in our logo, and its wood is tough. Strong, but flexible. That's why they make baseball bats out of it.

[PUNTO 2]
But that's not even the best part. Look closer. The leaves falling from our tree? They're house keys. Every one is a family that got home.

[PUNTO 3]
So when a deal gets hard, and they do, we bend. We don't break.

[CIERRE]
We're Ashwood. Brand new, born in the Poconos. (pausa) If you named a company after something in nature, what would it be?
