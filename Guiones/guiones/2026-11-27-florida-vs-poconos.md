# Florida vs. Poconos
| Plataforma | Duración | Objetivo | Audiencia | Formato | Modo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels/TikTok | <45 s | Viral + debate | Neoyorquinos que piensan mudarse | Comparación | Palabra por palabra |

## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
Everyone from New York moves to Florida. Here's why some of them pick the Poconos instead.

[PUNTO 1]
One: family. Florida is a flight away. The Poconos are a two-hour drive. Sunday dinner at mom's is still on.

[PUNTO 2]
Two: real seasons. Fall leaves, snow days, summer on the lake.

[PUNTO 3]
And the one people don't think about until they move: your job. Here, you can still get to the city when you need to.

[CIERRE]
Florida or the Poconos? Pick a side in the comments.

## Notas
- Texto en pantalla: `Florida 🌴 vs Poconos 🏔️`
- Pantalla dividida lado a lado; no hablar mal de Florida, solo comparar.
- "Pick a side" genera debate en comentarios.
