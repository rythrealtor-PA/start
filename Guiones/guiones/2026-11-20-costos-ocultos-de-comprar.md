## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
The down payment is not the only check you write when you buy a house. Here are the ones nobody warns you about.

[PUNTO 1]
Closing costs: lender fees, title insurance, the appraisal. Ask for an estimate in the first week, not the last.

[PUNTO 2]
Transfer tax: in Pennsylvania, buyers and sellers often split it. Ask how it works in your town.

[PUNTO 3]
And the one that surprises Poconos buyers the most: inspections. The house, the well water, the septic, sometimes radon. Each one is its own bill.

[CIERRE]
Want the full list for your budget before you start? Comment COSTS and I'll send it to you.

## Notas
- Texto en pantalla: `It's NOT just the down payment 💸`
- Visual: cheques o billetes que aparecen uno por uno (texto protagonista).
- Sin cifras de pagos mensuales ni tasas (reglas de publicidad de crédito); si quieres cifras, que las confirme un lender.
