## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
Pre-qualified and pre-approved are not the same thing. And sellers know the difference.

[PUNTO 1]
Pre-qualified usually means a lender looked at what you told them. It's quick, but it's an estimate.

[PUNTO 2]
Pre-approved means they actually checked your credit, your income and your documents.

[PUNTO 3]
So why does it matter? When a seller compares offers, the one with a pre-approval looks a lot stronger.

[CIERRE]
Want the list of documents lenders usually ask for? Comment READY and I'll send it to you.
