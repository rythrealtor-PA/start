# Kit de invierno para nuevos dueños en los Poconos
| Plataforma | Duración | Objetivo | Audiencia | Formato | Modo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reels/TikTok | <45 s | Viral educativo + leads | Compradores recientes y nuevos residentes | Lista práctica | Palabra por palabra |

## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
Just bought a house in the Poconos? Get these five things done before the first big storm.

[PUNTO 1]
One: a good shovel and ice melt, by the door, not in the shed.

[PUNTO 2]
Two: a plow service booked now. In January, everyone's calling.

[PUNTO 3]
Three: a full tank, if you heat with propane or oil, and your next delivery scheduled.

[PUNTO 4]
And four and five, the ones people forget: a backup plan for power outages, and knowing exactly where your main water shut-off is.

[CIERRE]
Save this for your first winter. Which one did you forget?
