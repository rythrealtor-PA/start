## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
Looking at a house in the Poconos this winter? Check these four things before you fall in love.

[PUNTO 1]
One: the heat. Is it oil, propane or electric? Ask what last winter actually cost.

[PUNTO 2]
Two: the driveway. If it's steep, ask who plows it and how much.

[PUNTO 3]
Three: look up at the roof. Big icicles along the gutters can mean heat is escaping.

[PUNTO 4]
And four, the one nobody checks: go down to the basement. Look for water stains or signs that pipes froze before.

[CIERRE]
Winter shows you the truth about a house. Comment WINTER and I'll send you the full checklist.
