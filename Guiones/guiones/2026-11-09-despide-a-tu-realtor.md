## 🎣 Hook (0–4 s)
- **Visual:** el broker señala a cámara y levanta 3 dedos; corte seco a un plano más cerrado.
- **Dice:** "If your realtor does any of these three things… fire them."
- **Texto en pantalla:** `🚩🚩🚩 Fire your realtor if…`

## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
If your realtor does any of these three things… fire them.

[PUNTO 1]
One: they can't explain how they priced your home in under sixty seconds. If they can't explain it, they guessed.

[PUNTO 2]
Two, and this one's worse: you only hear from them when they need a signature. You should never have to ask, "any updates?"

[PUNTO 3]
And three, the one nobody talks about: they rush you to sign. Your deadline should be yours, not their paycheck's.

[CIERRE]
And if your agent does all three… (pausa) you know where to find us. Ashwood.

## Notas de edición
- Palabras clave a resaltar: fire, guessed, signature, rush, deadline, Ashwood
- Gráficos: tres "🚩" que entran uno por uno (texto protagonista) · nada de caras ni nombres de agentes reales
- Firma de marca: el logo entra en "you know where to find us" + cierre de logo de 2.5 s
