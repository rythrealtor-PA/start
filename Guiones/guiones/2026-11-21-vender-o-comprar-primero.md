## Texto para grabar (teleprompter)
[HOOK]
Sell first or buy first? Get it wrong, and you either pay two mortgages or have nowhere to live.

[PUNTO 1]
Sell first, and you know exactly how much you have to spend. But you might need a place to stay in between.

[PUNTO 2]
Buy first, and you only move once. But you could be paying for two homes at the same time.

[PUNTO 3]
But there's a third option most sellers never hear about. A rent-back: you sell, you close, and you stay in the house for a few weeks while you buy the next one.

[CIERRE]
Planning a move? Comment MOVE and I'll help you map out the order.
