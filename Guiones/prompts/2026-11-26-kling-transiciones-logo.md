# Kling: 3 transiciones (logo → lobo translúcido → montaña real → paisaje)

Imágenes: 1 = logo completo · 2 = lobo translúcido · 3 = montaña real con lobo fantasma · 4 = paisaje final sin logo.
Clips: 1→2, 2→3, 3→4 (Start frame + End frame), 9:16, 5 s cada uno, modo Professional.

Antes: las imágenes 1 y 2 tienen el mismo encuadre, así que la cámara no puede avanzar entre ellas. Recorta la imagen 2 unos 10–15% centrada en la silueta de la montaña (detrás del techo) y usa ese recorte como cuadro final del clip 1.

## Clip 1 (1 → 2)
Cinematic slow dolly-in toward the carved mountain silhouette in the wolf's mane, at a constant speed. The wolf's carved fur begins to move gently in a soft breeze, like real fur, as the wolf slowly becomes translucent and starts to fade. The mountain silhouette above the roof glows softly and becomes the focus of the shot. The golden sunrise grows stronger, with soft light rays through the misty forest and floating dust particles. Smooth, continuous, photorealistic, no cuts.

## Clip 2 (2 → 3)
The camera keeps moving forward at the same constant speed toward the mountain silhouette. The silhouette slowly turns into a real, photorealistic forested mountain with twin rocky peaks, keeping its exact shape. The wolf's fur keeps flowing slowly in the wind as the wolf fades into a soft, ghostly mist in the sky. The carved tree and the house dissolve into golden fog while real pine trees rise in from both sides. The sun stays in the same place with warm light rays. Seamless cinematic transformation, one continuous shot.

## Clip 3 (3 → 4)
The camera continues forward and slightly upward like a drone, at the same constant speed, toward the real mountain. The last ghostly strands of the wolf's fur drift away and vanish into the sunlit mist, together with the carved leaves and the house. Behind the mountain, the landscape opens up: layered forested ridges, rolling mist and soft clouds. Golden sunrise rays sweep across the peaks. Smooth aerial drone movement, cinematic, photorealistic, one continuous shot.

## Negative prompt (los 3 clips)
camera shake, sudden zoom, speed changes, cuts, flicker, warping, distortion, melting shapes, extra animals, duplicate wolf, text, letters, watermark, blurry

## Unir los clips
- Los 3 a la misma velocidad; si uno se siente más lento, ajústalo en el editor.
- Si Kling frena al final de un clip, recorta los últimos 3–5 cuadros y los primeros del siguiente; si aún se nota, un fundido de 2–4 cuadros.
- Sonido: viento suave + aullido en el clip 1, "whoosh" en el clip 2, música que crece en el clip 3.
