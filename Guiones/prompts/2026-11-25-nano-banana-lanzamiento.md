# Prompts Nano Banana — video de lanzamiento Ashwood

Cómo usarlos:
1. Sube siempre la imagen del logo (o la imagen anterior) junto con el prompt.
2. Genera en orden: cada imagen nueva se hace EDITANDO la anterior ("Using this image…"), así el estilo no cambia.
3. Formato vertical 9:16. Sin texto en las imágenes: el texto se pone después en el editor.
4. Guarda inicio y final de cada escena para animarlas con imagen→video.

## Bloque de estilo (pegar al final de cada prompt)
Cinematic painterly realism, warm golden-hour light, soft morning mist, rich forest greens and warm amber tones, gentle film grain, shallow depth of field, vertical 9:16 composition, no text, no letters, no watermark.

## 1 · Logo → escena cinematográfica (inicio)
Transform this logo into a cinematic illustrated scene while keeping the exact same composition, shapes and colors: the ash tree, the house, the wolf and the mountain shapes in the wolf's mane must stay in the same positions. Set it at dawn on a dark background that slowly fades into soft light, with a light breeze moving the tree's leaves. [bloque de estilo]

## 2 · Las hojas se vuelven llaves
Using this image, make a few leaves falling from the ash tree turn into small golden house keys in mid-air, catching the sunrise light. Some are still leaves, some are halfway between leaf and key, some are fully keys. Keep everything else exactly the same. [bloque de estilo]

## 3 · La casa brilla
Using this image, add a quick warm glint of light sweeping across the house, as if the first sunbeam just hit it. Warm light glows from the windows. Keep everything else exactly the same. [bloque de estilo]

## 4 · El lobo aúlla al amanecer
Using this image, show the wolf rising from behind the house, head raised, howling toward the sunrise on the horizon. Its fur is lit by golden light from the front. Keep the wolf's shape and the mountains in its mane exactly as in the logo. [bloque de estilo]

## 5 · El lobo se desvanece
Using this image, make the wolf slowly fade into the morning mist, semi-transparent, so the house and the tree become the clear focus. Keep everything else exactly the same. [bloque de estilo]

## 6 · Zoom a las montañas de la melena (cuadro de transición)
Close-up of the mountain shapes inside the wolf's mane from this image, filling the frame. The ridgeline silhouette sits in the lower third, with sunrise sky above. Keep the exact silhouette of the mountains from the logo. [bloque de estilo]

## 7 · Montaña real con la misma silueta (solo si no tienes video de dron)
Photorealistic aerial view of the Pocono Mountains in Pennsylvania at sunrise, rolling forested ridgelines with low mist in the valleys, autumn colors. Match the ridgeline silhouette of the reference image exactly, in the same position in the frame, so it can be used as a seamless transition. Vertical 9:16, no text.

## 8 · Río Delaware (solo si no tienes video real)
Photorealistic view of a wide, calm river winding between forested mountains at sunrise, soft mist on the water, warm golden light, in the style of the Delaware Water Gap in Pennsylvania. Vertical 9:16, no text.
(Mejor usar video real o stock con licencia: es más auténtico.)

## 9 · Casa cálida al atardecer
Photorealistic cozy wooden home in a Pocono Mountains forest at dusk, warm light glowing from every window, a stone path to the front porch, light snow on the ground, smoke rising from the chimney, inviting and calm. Vertical 9:16, no people, no text.
(Imagen ilustrativa: no presentarla como una propiedad real en venta.)

## 10 · Fondo para el logo final
Using the logo, place it centered on a deep dark green background with a soft golden glow behind it and faint mist, leaving clean empty space below the logo for text to be added later. Keep the logo exactly the same, no extra text. Vertical 9:16.

## Extra · Fotos del equipo con el mismo estilo
Using this photo, keep the person's face, hair, clothes and expression exactly the same. Only change the background to a soft, blurred Pocono forest at golden hour and match the lighting to warm sunrise tones. Professional portrait, vertical 9:16, no text.

# Transición logo → montaña real (versión mejorada, a partir de la imagen del logo en 3D)
Imagen base: logo tallado con marco de piedra, fresno, casa de madera con ventana encendida, lobo translúcido aullando, bosque y amanecer a la derecha.

## Imagen A · Cuadro inicial (editar la imagen base)
Using this image, keep everything exactly the same: the stone ring frame, the carved ash tree, the wooden house with the glowing window, the translucent howling wolf, the misty pine forest, the light rays and the sunrise on the right. Only move the camera slightly closer, about a 15% push-in, centered on the carved mountain silhouette in the wolf's mane just above the roof peak. Same lighting, same sun position, same colors, same horizon line. Photorealistic, cinematic, vertical 9:16, no new elements, no text.

## Imagen B · Cuadro final (editar la Imagen A)
Using this image as the reference for composition and light, show what the camera sees after passing through the logo: the carved mountain silhouette has become a real, photorealistic Pocono mountain ridge in exactly the same position, size and shape, covered in pine and hardwood forest, with morning mist in the valleys. Keep the same sunrise in the same spot on the right, the same golden light rays through the trees, the same misty pine forest in the foreground and the same sky colors. Completely remove the stone frame, the carved tree, the house, the wolf and the letters, so only the real landscape remains. Cinematic, photorealistic, like a frame from a nature documentary, vertical 9:16, no text, no watermark.

## Imagen intermedia (opcional, para una transición todavía más suave)
Using this image, show the halfway moment of the transition: the camera is closer, the carved mountain silhouette is turning into real rock and forest, and the stone frame, the carved tree, the house and the wolf are softening and dissolving into golden mist and light. Keep the same sun, light rays, trees and horizon line. Photorealistic, cinematic, vertical 9:16, no text.

## Prompt para el video (imagen→video: inicio = A, final = B)
Slow, smooth cinematic dolly-in moving forward through the circular stone frame. As the camera passes through, the carved elements dissolve into golden morning mist, and the carved mountain silhouette seamlessly transforms into the real Pocono mountain ridge. The sun stays in the same place, with soft light rays and floating dust particles. One continuous shot, no cuts, no camera shake, constant speed, realistic, vertical 9:16, 5–8 seconds.

## Si Nano Banana se desvía
- Mueve el sol: "Keep the sun at exactly the same height and position as the reference image."
- Cambia la montaña: "The ridgeline must match the carved mountain silhouette exactly."
- Agrega texto: "No letters, no words, no logo text."
- Si tienes el video del dron: usa su primer cuadro como Imagen B (o dáselo a Nano Banana como referencia: "Match the ridgeline, lighting and colors of this photo").
