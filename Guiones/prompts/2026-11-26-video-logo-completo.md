# Prompt de video: logo completo → árbol → lobo → montañas

Imagen inicial: el logo completo. Imagen final (si tu generador lo permite): el cuadro 2 de la transición (montaña real).

## Prompt principal (un solo clip de ~10 s)
Cinematic single continuous shot, vertical 9:16, starting on this full logo, realistic and smooth.
0–1 s: hold on the full logo in warm sunrise light.
1–2 s: the camera glides quickly but smoothly toward the carved ash tree on the left.
2–4 s: close on the tree, its branches sway in a light breeze and a few leaves detach and fall slowly, catching the golden light.
4–5 s: the camera smoothly pulls back to the original full-logo framing.
5–6 s: the camera pushes forward toward the wolf.
6–8 s: the wolf comes to life, realistic fur moving in the wind, it raises its head and howls toward the sunrise.
8–10 s: the camera keeps moving forward toward the mountain silhouette in the wolf's mane; the logo dissolves into golden mist and the silhouette becomes the real Pocono Mountains at sunrise.
Smooth easing between moves, no cuts, no camera shake, the sun stays in the same place, soft mist and floating dust particles, no text.

Opcional (historia del logo): en 2–4 s agregar "some of the falling leaves turn into small golden house keys".
