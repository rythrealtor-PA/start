# Viral Video Scripts — Brokerage Launch Playbook

**Goal:** Make a brand-new brokerage known and recognized in its market.
**Format:** Broker talking to the camera (9:16 vertical; Reels, TikTok, Shorts).
**Fill-ins:** Replace `[BROKERAGE]`, `[BROKER NAME]`, `[CITY]`, `[NEIGHBORHOOD]` and every number in brackets with your real details. Never film a made-up story or number. The real version always performs better and keeps you compliant.

---

## 1. What the competitive research says

### What's working right now
| Pattern | Why it works | Who does it well |
|---|---|---|
| **Personality over polish.** Phone-shot, face-to-camera, real opinions | People follow people, not logos. Raw video reads as honest. | Glennda Baker (went from 122 to 565K+ TikTok followers in about a year with one rule: *shoot everything on video*) |
| **Polarizing takes.** Some people love it, some hate it, and both comment. | Comments and stitches push the video to more people | Tatiana Londono (about 2.7M followers) says she balances love and hate on purpose |
| **The leader is the face of the brand** | Recognition attaches to a person first, then to the company | Ryan Serhant: SERHANT is built on the founder's personal account |
| **Hyper-local hooks.** "If you live in [CITY]…" | They filter for your real market, and local viewers save and share | Top local agents everywhere |
| **Cost-of-mistake and insider truth** | Fear of losing money beats "5 tips for buyers" every time | Consumer-advocate agents |
| **Series with a recurring format** | Viewers come back for the next episode, and that's how recognition builds | "Day X of…" build-in-public creators |

### What's NOT working (where your competitors are stuck)
- "Welcome to this beautiful 3-bed, 2-bath…" openings. Generic, and swiped away.
- "5 tips for first-time buyers." Every agent posts the same list.
- Over-produced brand videos with music and no face. They look like ads, so people skip them.
- Listing-only feeds. They only interest people buying that exact house.

### Benchmarks to beat
- **3-second hold:** aim for **≥ 70–80%** of viewers still watching at 3 seconds. If more than half are gone by 3 seconds, the algorithm treats the video as dead.
- **Ideal length:** Reels **15–30s** is the sweet spot. TikTok real estate content peaks **under 90s**.
- These are industry-blog benchmarks, not platform data. Your own analytics become the real baseline after about 10 posts.

### Your unfair advantage as a NEW brokerage
Your competitors are established. That makes them look safe, and it also makes them boring. You have the one story they can't tell: **the underdog building something from zero, in public.** Every script below uses that.

## 2. The 5 rules of every hook (read before filming)

1. **Words and motion in the first frame.** Start mid-sentence or mid-movement (walking in, door opening, putting the phone down). No "Hi guys, I'm…"
2. **Text on screen = the hook.** About half of viewers watch muted. Burn the hook text into the top third of the frame.
3. **Open a loop.** Make a promise or ask a question that only gets answered at the end.
4. **Something changes every 3–5 seconds.** A jump cut, a zoom-in, a prop, a location change, a new text pop.
5. **The ending loops back to the start.** The last line should flow into the first line, so replays count as watch time.

## 3. The Scripts

> Timing assumes about 2.5 spoken words per second (normal camera pace). Read it out loud once with a timer before filming.

### SCRIPT 1 — "Everyone Said This Was a Mistake" (Founder Story)
**Duration:** 45–50 seconds
**Purpose:** The anchor brand video. Post it first and pin it.
**Viral driver:** Underdog story, curiosity, a loop ending.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Broker unlocks the door of the (empty or new) office and walks in toward the camera.
- **Say:** *"Everyone told me opening my own brokerage was a mistake."*
- **On-screen text:** `Everyone said this was a mistake 👇`

**BODY**
| Time | Visual | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 3–9s | Jump cut, closer framing | "'The market's slow.' 'The big names own this city.' 'Just stay where you are.'" |
| 9–12s | Pause, half smile | "And honestly? They had a point." |
| 12–24s | Sitting at desk, serious | "But after [X] years in this business, I kept seeing the same thing: clients treated like a number. Deals rushed to hit a quota. And nobody actually explaining where your money goes." |
| 24–32s | Stand up, logo on wall/laptop visible | "So I built the company I wish existed. **[BROKERAGE].** You'll always know what's happening, what it costs, and who's working for you." |
| 32–40s | Walking toward camera | "We're brand new in [CITY]. Follow along, because I'm going to show you everything. The wins, the mistakes, all of it." |
| 40–45s | Stops, looks into lens | "Was it a mistake? **Ask me in a year.**" *(loops back to the hook)* |

**Caption:** `Day 1 of the rest of my career. Was it a mistake? You tell me in a year 👇 #[city]realestate #realestate #smallbusiness`
**Alt hooks to A/B test:**
- "I walked away from [a big brokerage / a safe career] to do this."
- "This is what it looks like to start a real estate company from zero."

### SCRIPT 2 — "Day [#] of Building a Brokerage From Zero" (Recurring Series)
**Duration:** 25–35 seconds per episode
**Purpose:** The recognition engine. People come back to see "what day are we on?"
**Viral driver:** Build-in-public, a running counter, a cliffhanger every episode.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Broker in car / walking / at desk. Counter graphic in the corner.
- **Say:** *"Day [1] of building a real estate brokerage from zero. Clients: zero."*
- **On-screen text (stays on screen the whole video):** `DAY [1] | Clients: [0] | Homes sold: [0]`

**BODY (template)**
| Time | Script |
|---|---|
| 3–15s | "Today I [one real thing: signed the office lease / got our license framed / had my first listing appointment / got rejected by a seller]." |
| 15–25s | "What I learned: [one honest sentence]." |
| 25–30s | "Tomorrow: [tease the next thing]. Follow so you see if it works." |

**Day 1 example:** *"Day 1 of building a real estate brokerage from zero. Clients: zero. Today I signed the paperwork that made [BROKERAGE] official. Lesson one: nobody hands you trust. You earn it, one person at a time. Tomorrow I'm knocking on 50 doors in [NEIGHBORHOOD] to see if anyone even knows who we are. Follow, because this could go very badly."*

**Episode bank:** first logo reveal · first sign in the ground · first rejection · first listing · first agent who joins · first closing (huge episode) · "what it costs to start a brokerage" (the numbers) · door-knocking reactions · open house with zero visitors vs. packed.

> 🔑 **Most important rule:** Post the series consistently, 3–5 times a week. The series only works if it never disappears.

### SCRIPT 3 — "I Own the Brokerage, So I Can Say This" (Insider Truths)
**Duration:** 40–45 seconds
**Purpose:** Authority plus controversy. Starts debates in the comments.
**Viral driver:** Insider secrets, a polarizing take, a comment-bait ending.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Broker leans into the camera, lowers voice slightly.
- **Say:** *"I own a real estate brokerage, so I'm allowed to say this: most agents won't tell you these 3 things."*
- **On-screen text:** `3 things agents won't tell you 🤫`

**BODY**
| Time | Visual | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 3–14s | Big "1" text pop | "**One:** commission is negotiable. Always has been. There is no rate set by law. Anyone who says 'that's just the standard' is choosing a number." |
| 14–25s | Jump cut, "2" | "**Two:** that online home estimate is not your home's price. Your price is what a real buyer will pay, this month, on your street." |
| 25–36s | Jump cut, "3" | "**Three:** if your agent is rushing you to sign, ask why. Your deadline should be your deadline, not their paycheck's." |
| 36–43s | Smile, point at camera | "There's a fourth one, and it makes agents really mad. Comment **'PART 2'** and I'll post it." |

**Caption:** `Agents are going to be mad at me for this one 😅 Comment PART 2 👇`
**Follow-up:** Actually post Part 2 (for example: "you can interview 3 agents before choosing one, and you should"). Reply to the top comment with a video reply.

### SCRIPT 4 — "The Real Math on Your Agent's Paycheck" (Commission Transparency)
**Duration:** 45–55 seconds
**Purpose:** The highest-share topic in real estate since the commission settlement. Positions [BROKERAGE] as the transparent brokerage.
**Viral driver:** A shocking number, a money breakdown, "I didn't know that" shares.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Broker writes a big dollar figure on a whiteboard or notepad, then crosses it out.
- **Say:** *"You think your agent made [$10,000] on your house? Let me show you the real number."*
- **On-screen text:** `What your agent ACTUALLY takes home 💸`

**BODY** *(example numbers only. Use a real, anonymized deal. Always say "example".)*
| Time | Visual | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 3–10s | Writing on board | "Example: a [$400,000] house. Let's say the agent's side is [$10,000]. Remember, every commission is negotiable." |
| 10–20s | Arrow, subtract | "First, the brokerage gets its share. On a [70/30] split, the agent is now at [$7,000]." |
| 20–32s | Subtract again | "Then come MLS dues, licensing, insurance, marketing, photos, gas. Then taxes. Because agents are self-employed, nobody withholds anything." |
| 32–40s | Circle final number | "Real take-home on this example: around [$4,500]. For a deal that can take [3 months] of work." |
| 40–52s | Put marker down, face camera | "Why is a broker telling you this? Because at [BROKERAGE], you see every number **before** you sign. No surprises. That's the whole point." |

**Caption:** `Most people have no idea how this works. Save this before you buy or sell 📌`
**⚠️ Compliance:** Never say "the standard commission is X%". Talking about a "standard rate" in public can create antitrust risk. Keep every number labeled as an example, and say "negotiable" on camera.

### SCRIPT 5 — "If I Were Buying in [CITY] Right Now…" (Local Authority)
**Duration:** 50–60 seconds
**Purpose:** Makes [BROKERAGE] the local expert. Gets saves and shares from people moving to the city.
**Viral driver:** A local call-out hook (it filters for your market), a specific budget, and "the one I'd avoid".

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Green-screen effect with a map of [CITY] behind the broker.
- **Say:** *"If I were buying in [CITY] with [$350K] right now, here are the 3 areas I'd look at… and the one I'd skip."*
- **On-screen text:** `Buying in [CITY] in [2027]? Watch this first 📍`

**BODY**
| Time | Visual | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 3–17s | Point to area 1 on map | "**[NEIGHBORHOOD 1].** Prices are still around [$X], and [new development / new road / new employer] is coming. Early buyers usually win here." |
| 17–30s | Area 2 | "**[NEIGHBORHOOD 2].** Homes are sitting [X] days on the market, so buyers have room to negotiate." |
| 30–42s | Area 3 | "**[NEIGHBORHOOD 3].** Best value per square foot I've seen this year: about [$X]." |
| 42–52s | Area 4, X mark | "And the one I'd skip *right now*: **[AREA].** Not because it's bad. Prices jumped [X%] and I think you'd be overpaying today." |
| 52–58s | Face camera | "Want the full list with real numbers? Comment **'[CITY]'** and I'll send it to you." |

**Caption:** `Save this if you're moving to [CITY] 📍 Comment [CITY] for the full list`
**⚠️ Fair Housing:** Talk only about **prices, inventory, days on market, commute, and development**. Never describe an area by who lives there. Never call an area "safe," "family-friendly," or "good schools / bad schools." Those words can be treated as steering.

### SCRIPT 6 — "This One Sentence Almost Cost My Client [$X]" (Story / Cost of Mistake)
**Duration:** 40–45 seconds
**Purpose:** Shows expertise through a story, not a sales pitch.
**Viral driver:** A money-loss hook, suspense, a "check yours" call to action.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Broker holds up a printed contract and slaps a highlighter mark on one line.
- **Say:** *"This one sentence almost cost my client [$18,000]."*
- **On-screen text:** `1 sentence = [$18,000] mistake 😳`

**BODY** *(use a REAL story, with client permission or fully anonymized)*
| Time | Script |
|---|---|
| 3–14s | "My client was [buying/selling] a home in [CITY]. Everything looked perfect. Then I read [the inspection contingency / the appraisal clause / the closing date line]…" |
| 14–28s | "It said [plain-English explanation of the problem]. That meant if [X happened], they would [lose the deposit / pay for repairs / lose the house]." |
| 28–38s | "We caught it, we changed one line, and they [saved $X / closed on time]." |
| 38–45s | "If you're under contract right now, check this line in yours tonight. And if you're not sure what it means, send it to me. I'll look, for free." |

**Caption:** `Check your contract tonight. Seriously. 📄 #realestatetips #[city]homes`

### SCRIPT 7 — "If Your Realtor Does These 3 Things, Fire Them" (Red Flags)
**Duration:** 30–35 seconds
**Purpose:** Bold, shareable, and it quietly says "we're different".
**Viral driver:** A strong polarizing statement, "tag someone" shares, a punchline ending.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Broker points at the camera, holding up 3 fingers.
- **Say:** *"If your realtor does any of these 3 things… fire them."*
- **On-screen text:** `🚩🚩🚩 Fire your realtor if…`

**BODY**
| Time | Script |
|---|---|
| 3–11s | "🚩 **One:** they can't explain how they priced your home in under 60 seconds. If they can't explain it, they guessed." |
| 11–19s | "🚩 **Two:** they only text you when they need a signature. You should never have to ask 'any updates?'" |
| 19–27s | "🚩 **Three:** they push one lender or one inspector and never give you options. You deserve choices, not a package deal." |
| 27–33s | Pause, smirk. "And if your agent does all three… *(logo pops up)*… you know where to find me." |

**Caption:** `Tag someone who needs to hear this 🚩`

### SCRIPT 8 — "Who We Are in 20 Seconds" (Pinned Profile Video)
**Duration:** 20 seconds
**Purpose:** Turns a new profile visitor into a follower. Pin it as the 1st video.

**🎣 HOOK (0–2s)**
- **Say:** *"If you just found this page, here's who we are in 20 seconds."*
- **On-screen text:** `New here? Start with this 👋`

**BODY:** *"I'm [BROKER NAME], founder of [BROKERAGE], the newest brokerage in [CITY]. Three promises: you'll always know where your money goes, you'll always reach a real person, and we'll never rush you into a deal. On this page I show the real side of real estate: the numbers, the mistakes, and what it takes to build this from zero. Hit follow. Day [#] starts tomorrow."*

### SCRIPT 9 — "Guess the Price" (Engagement Game)
**Duration:** 25–30 seconds
**Purpose:** Pure engagement. Comments explode because everyone wants to guess.
**Viral driver:** Interactive, a delayed answer that keeps people watching to the end.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Broker standing in front of a [CITY] home (your own listing or open house).
- **Say:** *"Guess the price of this [CITY] home. I bet you're wrong."*
- **On-screen text:** `Guess the price 👇 (answer at the end)`

**BODY:** Fast cuts of 3–4 rooms, about 3 seconds each, with the broker giving one specific detail per room ("[X] square feet", "built [year]", "[feature]"). At 22s: *"Comment your guess. Answer in 3… 2… 1…"* Reveal the price with a sound effect, then: *"Closer than you thought? Follow for the next one."*

**⚠️** Use only listings you have the right to show (your own, or with the listing agent's permission per your MLS rules).

## 5. Filming & Editing Checklist
- [ ] Vertical 9:16, phone at eye level, broker looking **into the lens**, not at the screen
- [ ] Clip-on mic. Bad audio kills more videos than bad video does.
- [ ] Natural light facing the broker (window in front, not behind)
- [ ] Captions burned in, hook text in the top third, kept clear of the app buttons
- [ ] Cut every pause and every "um". No dead air.
- [ ] A visual change every 3–5 seconds (cut, zoom, text pop, B-roll)
- [ ] Brokerage name and license info as required by your state's advertising rules (in the bio, the caption, or a small end card)
- [ ] Same outfit color or signature item every time. A consistent look helps people recognize you.
- [ ] Film 4–6 videos in one session (batch) to stay consistent

## 6. 30-Day Launch Rollout
| Week | Post |
|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Script 1 (Founder Story) → pin Script 8 → Series Day 1 → Script 7 (Red Flags) |
| **Week 2** | Series Days 2–4 → Script 3 (Insider Truths) → reply video to "PART 2" comments |
| **Week 3** | Script 4 (Commission Math) → Script 5 (Local Authority) → Series Days 5–7 |
| **Week 4** | Script 6 (Contract Story) → Script 9 (Guess the Price) → Series Days 8–10 → re-post the best performer with a new hook |

**Cadence:** 4–5 posts a week. Cross-post the same video to Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels.
**Daily 15 minutes:** reply to every comment, ideally with **video replies**. Each reply is free extra content.

## 7. KPIs to Track Weekly
| Metric | Target | If it's low |
|---|---|---|
| 3-second hold | ≥ 70% | Rewrite the hook. The opening is the problem. |
| Average watch % | ≥ 50% | Cut the script 20% shorter |
| Shares + saves per 1K views | Rising week over week | Add more money and local value |
| Follows per 1K views | Rising | Make the series and the CTA clearer |
| DMs / "[CITY]" comments | Track each one | That's brand recognition turning into leads |
