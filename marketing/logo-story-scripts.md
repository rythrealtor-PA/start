# Logo Story Scripts — "The Wolf, the Ash Tree & the Keys"

**Goal:** Turn the logo into the brokerage's origin story, and get people talking about it.
**Format:** Broker talking to camera, with zoom-ins on the logo (9:16 vertical; Reels, TikTok, Shorts).
**Fill-ins:** `[BROKERAGE]`, `[TOWN]`, `[#]`, `[LAST NAME]`.

---

## The symbolism (your source of truth)
| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🌳 **Ash tree** | The tree the brokerage grows from |
| 🍂🔑 **Falling leaves are house keys** | Every leaf that falls is a family getting home |
| 🌅 **Wolf faces the SUNRISE, not the sunset** | Our backs are never turned on progress |
| 👻 **The wolf is fading** | We're a small presence in the client's whole life story. The client is the central focus. |
| 🏔️ **Poconos mountains in the wolf's mane** | Where we were founded. It's in our DNA. |

*Optional ash-tree lines, only if they fit your brand:* ash wood is known for being strong but flexible (it's used for baseball bats and tool handles): "we bend, we don't break". In Norse mythology the "tree of life," Yggdrasil, is an ash.

---

## Why a logo video can go viral
1. **The "hidden meaning" format is proven.** People love "you never noticed this" logo reveals (like the FedEx arrow). Viewers **pause and rewatch** to find the details, and rewatches are the strongest watch-time signal there is.
2. **It reaches beyond real estate.** Design and branding audiences share logo-meaning content, so the video can travel past homebuyers.
3. **It turns comments into a game.** "I found 3!" "I missed the keys 😭". Every answer is a comment.
4. **One logo becomes 5+ videos.** Don't explain everything every time. Each symbol can carry its own video.

## OPTION 1 — "4 Hidden Details" Challenge ⭐ POST THIS FIRST
**Duration:** 40–50 seconds
**Viral driver:** Interactive challenge, pause-and-rewatch, comment game.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** The full logo fills the screen. The broker's voice comes in immediately (broker on camera in the corner, or a cut to the broker at 3s).
- **Say:** *"There are 4 hidden details in our logo. Most people only find one."*
- **On-screen text:** `4 hidden details 👀 How many can you find?`

**BODY**
| Time | Visual | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 3–6s | Logo held still, with a 3-2-1 timer graphic | "Pause it. Look. …Okay." |
| 6–14s | Slow zoom on the falling leaves; one leaf morphs into a key | "**One:** the leaves falling from this ash tree aren't leaves. They're house keys. Every one is a family that got home." |
| 14–22s | Zoom on the wolf's eyes and the sun | "**Two:** the wolf is facing the *sunrise*, not the sunset. Our backs are never turned on progress." |
| 22–30s | Trace the mountain outline inside the mane | "**Three:** look inside the mane. Those are the Pocono Mountains, where we started [BROKERAGE]." |
| 30–42s | Pull back. Broker on camera, softer tone | "And **four**, the one almost nobody catches: the wolf is *fading*. Because in your life story, we're only a small part. You're the main character." |
| 42–47s | Logo full again | "So how many did you get? **Be honest.** 👇" *(loops back to the logo)* |

**Caption:** `Most people miss #4 👀 How many did you find? #poconos #logodesign #hiddenmeaning #realestate #poconosrealestate`
**Pin a comment:** `Hint: look at the leaves again 🔑`

## OPTION 2 — "Our Wolf Is Fading. That's Not a Mistake."
**Duration:** 30–35 seconds
**Viral driver:** It looks like an error, which makes people curious, and the answer lands emotionally.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Extreme close-up of the faded part of the wolf, then slowly pull back.
- **Say:** *"Look closely. Our wolf is fading. That's not a printing mistake."*
- **On-screen text:** `Our wolf is disappearing… on purpose`

**BODY**
| Time | Script |
|---|---|
| 3–14s | "When you buy a home, it's one chapter in a really long story. First steps in the hallway. Birthdays in the kitchen. Maybe one day, handing those keys to your own kids." |
| 14–22s | "We're there for a few months of that story. That's it." |
| 22–30s | "So we made ourselves fade, on purpose, so *you* stay the center of the picture." |
| 30–35s | "Our job is to get you home, then step back. That's the [BROKERAGE] wolf." |

**Caption:** `You're the main character. We're just a chapter. 🐺`

## OPTION 3 — "Sunrise or Sunset?" (Comment Game)
**Duration:** 25–30 seconds
**Viral driver:** People comment their guess *before* the answer, which drives a flood of comments.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** A crop showing only the wolf and the sun.
- **Say:** *"Is our wolf looking at a sunrise or a sunset? Comment before I tell you."*
- **On-screen text:** `Sunrise or sunset? 🌅 Comment FIRST`

**BODY**
| Time | Script |
|---|---|
| 3–6s | *(3-second countdown timer on screen, no talking)* |
| 6–10s | "It's a **sunrise**. And that was a choice." |
| 10–20s | "A sunset means the day is over. You're looking back. A sunrise means *what's next*." |
| 20–28s | "We never turn our backs on progress. Not on the market, not on new ideas, and never on your next step." |

**Caption:** `Did you guess right? 🌅🐺 #poconos #realestate`

## OPTION 4 — "Another Leaf Fell" (Recurring Closing Series) 🔁
**Duration:** 20–30 seconds per episode
**Viral driver:** Emotional real moments, a running counter, a reason for people to keep coming back.
**Purpose:** The logo becomes a ritual. **Post one every time you close a deal.**

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** A real client's hands receiving the key in front of their new home (with permission).
- **Say (voiceover):** *"Every leaf that falls from our tree is a key."*
- **On-screen text:** `🍂🔑 Leaf #[1] has fallen`

**BODY**
| Time | Visual | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 3–8s | Logo zoom: one leaf falls and turns into a key | "This one belongs to the [LAST NAME] family." |
| 8–18s | Client unlocks the door, walks in, reacts (no script, just the real moment) | *(Natural sound. Let the moment breathe.)* |
| 18–25s | Broker to camera, in front of the house | "Leaf number [#]. Our tree is still young. Who's next?" |

**Running counter on every episode:** `Keys fallen: [#]`

> 💡 **Brand ritual (strongly recommended):** Give every client a real key on a **leaf-shaped keychain** at closing. Clients post it themselves, which spreads the logo for free, and people start asking "what's that leaf?"

## OPTION 5 — "Poconos People Only" (Local Pride)
**Duration:** 25–35 seconds
**Viral driver:** A local call-out hook. Locals share, tag friends, and feel represented.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** Zoom straight into the wolf's mane.
- **Say:** *"If you're from the Poconos, you'll recognize what's hiding in this wolf's mane."*
- **On-screen text:** `Poconos people only 🏔️ What's hidden in the mane?`

**BODY**
| Time | Visual | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 3–10s | Trace the mountain outline, then cross-fade to real footage of the same ridgeline | "Those are *our* mountains. [BROKERAGE] was born right here in [TOWN]." |
| 10–20s | Broker driving / at a local spot | "We're not a big chain that found the Poconos on a map. We live here. We drive these roads in the snow. We know these towns house by house." |
| 20–30s | Broker to camera | "So we put the mountains in the wolf, because they're in us too. Tag someone who's Poconos to the bone. 🏔️" |

**Caption:** `Born in the Poconos. Built for the Poconos. 🏔️🐺 Tag a local 👇 #poconos #poconomountains #poconosrealestate #pennsylvania`

## OPTION 6 — "I Came Here at 5 A.M. for a Reason" (Cinematic Sunrise Shoot)
**Duration:** 45–55 seconds
**Viral driver:** Stunning visuals and a mystery opener. Your best "brand film" that's still a talking-head video.
**Location:** A Poconos overlook facing **east**. Check the park or trail hours before you go.

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** It's still dark. The broker stands with their back to the camera, facing the horizon, then turns to the lens.
- **Say:** *"Most people film their brand video at sunset. I came here at 5 a.m. for a reason."*
- **On-screen text:** `Why we chose the sunrise 🌄`

**BODY**
| Time | Visual | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 3–12s | The sun starts breaking the horizon | "Our logo is a wolf. And that wolf faces the sunrise, never the sunset, because we never turn our backs on progress." |
| 12–20s | Wide shot of the mountains | "These mountains? They're in the wolf's mane. This is where [BROKERAGE] began." |
| 20–30s | Close-up of the broker holding a key | "The tree behind the wolf is an ash. Every leaf that falls is a key: a family getting home." |
| 30–42s | The sun fully up, broker looking out | "And the wolf fades out, on purpose. Because your life is the story. We're just here for one sunrise in it." |
| 42–50s | The logo animates in over the real sunrise | "[BROKERAGE]. A new day in Poconos real estate." |

**Caption:** `Every detail means something. 🌄🐺🔑 #poconos #sunrise #realestate #branding`
**Production:** Use a clip-on mic (wind!), lock exposure on the broker's face, and film extra B-roll of the sunrise for future videos.

## OPTION 7 — "We Almost Picked a Different Logo" (Behind the Scenes)
**Duration:** 30–40 seconds
**Viral driver:** Build-in-public, "which is better?" debate, design-community engagement.
**⚠️ Only use this if you have real drafts or sketches from the design process.**

**🎣 HOOK (0–3s)**
- **Visual:** The broker swipes through rejected drafts on a tablet or printed sheets.
- **Say:** *"We almost picked a completely different logo. Here's why we didn't."*
- **On-screen text:** `Logo #1 vs. the final 👀 Which would you pick?`

**BODY**
| Time | Script |
|---|---|
| 3–12s | "Draft one: [describe]. It looked good, but it didn't *say* anything." |
| 12–20s | "Then we asked: what do we actually believe? We'll never turn our backs on progress. The client is the story, not us. And we're Poconos to the core." |
| 20–32s | *(Reveal the final logo)* "So the wolf faces the sunrise. It fades so you stay the focus. The mountains live in its mane. And every falling leaf is a key." |
| 32–38s | "Which one would you have picked? Be honest." |

## Assets to prepare before filming
- [ ] **Layered logo files** (tree, leaves/keys, wolf, mane mountains, sun as separate PNG/SVG files) so the editor can zoom and animate each piece in CapCut or Canva
- [ ] A short animation of **one leaf falling and turning into a key**, used in every video as a visual signature
- [ ] A real **brass key + leaf keychain** as a prop (and as a closing gift)
- [ ] Real **Poconos ridgeline footage** that matches the mane outline
- [ ] A clip-on mic and captions burned in on every video

## Comment strategy
- Reply to "I found 3!" comments with a **video reply** showing the one they missed.
- Pin a hint comment on Option 1.
- When someone guesses "sunset" on Option 3, reply kindly with a video. Those replies become free follow-up content.
